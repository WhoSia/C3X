# P25 — Retrospective Conceptual Discovery and Prospective Scientific Boundaries

## The question raised at entry

Would distinguishing canonical position, provenance trajectory, and intervention world earlier have avoided P19–P24 bottlenecks?

This is an **unobserved counterfactual**, not a result. There is no experimental evidence that earlier recognition would have prevented P20's exact-event explosion, P21's fresh parent extinction, P22's routed-chain support failure or P23's activation transport failure. Some failure modes might have become easier to classify or detect earlier, but none may be reclassified post hoc.

## What is empirically grounded

P19 source acquisition already used a SHA-256 of a normalized four-field FEN under a 12–160-ply candidate window. This enabled strict historical and cross-source position exclusion but did not create separate diagnostic objects for trajectory provenance or the identity of the eventual counterfactual experiment.

P25 preserves this legacy selection policy and records three distinct objects:
- F: canonical position reuse (legacy exclusion binding);
- T: provenance/trajectory reuse (diagnostic, currently not measured);
- W: candidate pair + router grammar + board-intervention world (defined after pair/router freeze; currently not measured).

The source identity object I_s is independently necessary for source admissibility; it does not imply R=1 or A=1.

## Hypotheses to falsify prospectively

H1: Tier-F duplicate removal and Tier-T provenance overlap have distinguishable source/ecology distributions.

H2: Conditioning from source admissibility to executable routed support changes pre-outcome context distributions (phase/branching/tactics, engine, family), potentially altering the target population even without activation outcome conditioning.

H3: Once both population support and the activation model are frozen, a source-disjoint transport test can distinguish structural attrition from calibration failure.

H4: Exposing typed uncertainty to the PGN explanation renderer can prevent unsupported causal-language emissions without making the renderer an empirical causal test.

## Scientific claim ceiling

No one of H1–H4 is established by the new inventory/ledger code. Its CI demonstrates implementation fidelity on synthetic cases only. The research contribution requires a sealed, prospective source/engine intervention audit and a literature comparison against selection-biased causal transport and mechanistic interpretability work.
