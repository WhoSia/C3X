P5 extends P4 from upward evidence promotion into full authority revision dynamics.

## Scientific split

### Axis A — authority dynamics
Claims can be promoted, suspended, demoted, retracted, restored or context-branched. Ordinary evidence updates over one frozen regime should be confluent by final evidence set. Prospective order violations are intentionally noncommutative because provenance cannot be repaired by replaying history.

### Axis B — fresh local-certificate ecology
TWIC 1664 is frozen as a new source class. Its games are divided into two deterministic pre-outcome strata and passed through the inherited P16 exact-event FULL_BRIDGE contract. P5 does not change the P16 certificate definition to increase yield.

## Critical distinction

Fresh certificate count does not determine whether the authority-dynamics kernel is valid. Conversely, a correct state machine does not authorize a composition candidate without at least two complete independent local certificates.

## Composition boundary

Both TWIC strata share issue-level provenance. Therefore even two compatible fresh certificates from TWIC 1664 do not automatically satisfy P4 independent-provenance composition. They are evidence for conflict/compatibility structure and for future ecology design, not a transport law.
