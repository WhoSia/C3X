# C3X G10-P8 — Morphism-Conditioned Certificate Acquisition

P7 established a nondegenerate C/S/E certificate-morphism grammar but its untargeted prospective holdout produced no complete local certificates. P8 tests whether that grammar is useful **before** causal outcomes by turning only the board-trigger projection of nontrivial P7 orbits into an acquisition policy.

## Firewall
Target/control assignment occurs after baseline pair qualification and chain-candidate construction, but before chain qualification, factorial replay, response topology, certificate status or family-membership outcomes.

## Budget
TWIC 1657 and 1658 each contribute a deterministic 54-position candidate pool. At most 12 targeted and 12 matched controls per source proceed to causal replay.

## Distinct questions
1. Does morphism-conditioned acquisition improve complete-certificate yield?
2. Do any newly recovered complete certificates replicate the same P7 family across both sources and at least two engines?

The first can pass without the second. Neither authorizes transport.
