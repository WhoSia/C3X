### Methodological synthesis
- Probe accuracy ⇒ decodable information, not necessarily behavioral relevance; even targeted removals require a tested intervention and safeguards against accidental destruction of other useful information (Elazar versus Kumar).
- Plan explanation for **humans** targets the mismatch between models (Chakraborti) whereas C3X's scientific explanation of **physical search computation** targets event→ancestor→root action. These are different objects, even when the same UI makes both readable.
- Go keyword probes (Tomlin) can inspire a separately evaluated chess-commentary concept ontology but cannot be renamed a chess dataset.
- Sadikov's explicit goal and tactical/positional explanation highlights a stronger predecessor to P6 than superficial text generation.
