# C3X 0.30 R31 — A cached depth changes a later root choice with the same Kg7 return

R29-031 was already exposed. At attempt 14, the first call after 41…Kg7
was a scout with window [1950,1951] and entrance depth 12. Suppressing
the Bc6 hint triggered the scout's cut-node decrement for a missing
TT move, reducing depth from 12 to 10. Both scouts returned 1947.
The main-search exit wrote the same physical TT slot and attempted to
store no new move. The entry retained Bc6. The full key matched in both
writes, and the observer saw no other writes or refreshes in that
cluster before the PV re-search.

| Entry at PV consumption | Depth | Raw value | Bound | Static eval | Hint |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| Sham | 12 | 1947 | Upper | 2020 | Bc6 |
| Whole hint masked in scout | 10 | 1947 | Upper | 2020 | Bc6 |

The PV call had entrance depth 12 and window [1706,1951]. The two
natural worlds entered with the same generation and PV marker.
Because the raw value and bound happened to agree, crossing them yields
identity controls. The four identity controls left the checksummed
result data unchanged except for the recorded intervention fields.

## The depth swap and the fail-low heuristic

| Scout state and PV intervention | PV likely fail low | Kg7 PV return | Kg7 takes lead | Native final at 32,000 nodes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Ordinary, own entry | Yes | 1934 | Yes | g5 |
| Ordinary, substitute depth 10 | No | 1934 | Yes | Kg7 |
| Ordinary, force PV heuristic off | No | 1934 | Yes | Kg7 |
| Whole hint masked, own entry | No | 1952 | No | Rb2 |
| Whole hint masked, substitute depth 12 | Yes | 1952 | No | Rb2 |
| Whole hint masked, force PV heuristic on | Yes | 1952 | No | Rb2 |

The Stockfish 16 branch predicts fail-low in this PV call when a TT move
is present, the bound has an upper component, and stored depth is at
least search depth. The prediction controls a reduction discount in
the move loop. In the ordinary scout world, substituting depth 10 or
forcing the heuristic off enabled the discount. The first reduced
search for Rd7+ ran at depth 8 instead of 6. Kg7 still became the
temporary root leader at −1934. For the later g5 candidate in the same
attempt, the native return changed from −1884 to −1948 against the same
alpha −1934. The g5 candidate lost that comparison, so Kg7 remained
the leader through the budget limit. We traced the first changed
reductions and the root endpoint. We have yet to identify the exact
descendant or state update that made g5's later search differ.

In the full-mask world, the first PV reply Bc6 already reached the
beta threshold (returned 1952 with beta 1951). That invocation never
entered the late-move reduction branch, including after the reciprocal
depth swap. Both direct Boolean switches matched their corresponding
depth-cross arms on ordered LMR rows, the child chain, attempt exit,
final producer, last public report and normalized public stream.

This establishes a conditional route from a consumed TT depth via one
PV heuristic to a final recommendation. It leaves the full-mask
g5-to-Rb2 reversal unexplained by depth alone. The first Bc6 search
after the scout is the next point to trace. Its input includes more
than the entry for Kg7. Check Bc6's descendant reads, accumulated
history and first return before any later-move intervention.
Scores above are native search-window values.

## Budget sensitivity

Both scouts and the Kg7 candidate reproduced their respective prefixes
at each tested budget. The following are the actual bestmoves. The
24,000-node runs stopped inside attempt 14, the focused attempt.

| Node limit | Ordinary | Ordinary, heuristic off | Whole hint masked | Masked, heuristic on |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24,000 | Kg7 | Kg7 | g5 | g5 |
| 32,000 | g5 | Kg7 | Rb2 | Rb2 |
| 48,000 | g5 | Kg7 | Ke5 | Ke5 |
| 64,000 | g5 | Ke5 | Ke5 | Ke5 |

When attempt 14 completed, its leader was g5 in the ordinary control
and Kg7 with the heuristic off. Later searches could change the final
move. The comparison is conditioned on this one board and the
tested budgets.
