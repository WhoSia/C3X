# P19 — No advantage from unequal selective explanations
Question: does C3X genuinely reduce chess explanation errors compared with BinProp/CCC when both are required to speak about identical cases?
Naive error counting makes abstention almost free. P19 pairs by exact case_id and game_id and restricts reported risks to intersection of emitted cases. It reports intersection coverage, both errors, signed difference and refuses empty common output. Two disjoint abstaining methods cannot be ranked, even when each has perfect observed risk. This is only a descriptive shared-case comparison: it DOES NOT independently verify legal oracle or estimate external game-level confidence intervals. Full evaluation must ALSO report each method's own risk-coverage frontier and selectively different explanation difficulty; equal source/compute and raw atom labels. Do not claim C3X beat BinProp until a real new game-disjoint experiment is run.
