## Origin return

G9.5-P18 froze the exact `OWN|DISPREFERRED_FROM_LOSS` atom-law transport program. G10 does not rescue it. The founding question returns to the individual chess decision:

> Why this near-equal legal move rather than that one, and what exact evidence can legitimately enter an explanation?

G10 treats a complete **local** causal certificate as a potentially valuable terminal explanation object for one world, without pretending that it is automatically a cross-position or cross-engine law.

## Four-object loop

1. **Demand proposal** — generated from real PGN state plus bounded engine/chess evidence. Authority: question proposal only.
2. **Local causal certificate** — exact position × engine/runtime × candidate pair × intervention/falsifier scope. Authority: local engine-preference causality only.
3. **Chess-native consequence packet** — legal candidate/continuation/board consequences verified from the certificate. No human semantic label is inferred.
4. **Commentary handoff** — may mix causal and conventional atoms, but provenance remains machine-readable.

The intended loop is:

`real PGN → outcome-blind demand → Track A experiment → local certificate → chess-native consequence → Track B commentary`

A missing certificate does not block useful commentary; it blocks only causal wording.

## Frozen demand admission

P0 freezes a deliberately simple admission grammar before any new causal outcome:

- MultiPV ≥ 3;
- top-two bounded engine score gap ≤ 35 cp;
- played move appears within the first 3 candidates;
- top candidates are distinct;
- mate-like positions are excluded from the marginal-preference lane.

The grammar may later be rejected, but not tuned after seeing which admitted positions yield causal certificates.

## Success

P0 may PASS only if:

- real-PGN demand discovery is deterministic and outcome-blind;
- demand objects contain no causal-outcome fields;
- the canonical P16 local certificate normalizes and legally replays;
- chess-native consequence compression terminates in legal chess moves/boards;
- a historical local certificate round-trips through the authority-separated interface;
- a fresh PGN demand remains heuristic/question-only when no certificate exists;
- I0–I8 regression stays green.

## Authority ceiling

P0 can establish **dual-track loop realizability**. It cannot establish:

- a new transportable law;
- objective chess correctness from engine preference;
- human intention/cognition/strategic semantics;
- human utility.

## Return edge
