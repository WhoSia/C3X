# R25 — What the two actual replies change on the board

Both archived Qxe2 principal variations contain b4. Starting from the frozen legal history, replay Qxe2 and each first Black reply. Check if the resulting position is in check, enumerate White's legal responses to Bh2+, and identify the Black pieces that defend h2. Test whether b4 is legal immediately in either branch.

For each legal White response to the check, enumerate each ensuing legal Black move and test whether White can then make the exact b2b4 move. Publish the number of Black moves and the refuters. This is short-horizon legality of a concrete resource and its timing. Its survival over that horizon supplies no claim about best play, long-term strategic value, or what a neural feature represents.
