# C3X P25 — Counterfactual Reply Alignment Is Itself an Estimand

Date 2026-11-02. Critique of P24: a single reply_id on two different candidate-root boards is NOT automatically the same legal opponent action. In chess the set of opponent legal responses changes after a versus b. Naively joining by UCI or reply name can silently compare incomparable states. The P24 scalar counts must remain DECLARED_ORACLE_ONLY and cannot be promoted to real explanation robustness.

Two valid comparison estimands: (i) source-conditioned policies defined over common opponent intention or scenario categories whose equivalence is independently established; (ii) separate universal response domains for each candidate, comparing existence of candidate-specific good-plan policies under the same original player objective. For candidate x let R_x be its legal opponent response domain, T_x(r) own legal continuations, Q_x(r,t) independent chess predicate. Then P_x=1 iff for every r in R_x there exists a legal t in T_x(r) such that Q_x(r,t)=1. The claim P_a=1 and P_b=0 is comparative but DOES NOT imply a more favorable Stockfish evaluation unless chess criterion Q is sufficiently tied to meaningful outcomes and preference stability is established.

Most demanding falsifier: find s,a,b with equal static labels, P_a=1, P_b=0, but independent outcome measure shows b better or a tie. Such cases must remain negative evidence for naive plan-affordance superiority. Conversely find s,a,b with stable preference a but P_a=P_b: the proposed plan predicate does not explain the preference and should abstain, not improvise another postoutcome predicate.

Scientific decision: stop using correspondence of identical reply IDs as a proxy for counterfactual world matching. Prefer separately enumerated verified legal reply universes, fixed plan semantics, and prospective blind independent judgment of strategic relevance. P24 is a prototype for data aligned by an external oracle, not a native chess causal evaluator. No engine results claimed.
