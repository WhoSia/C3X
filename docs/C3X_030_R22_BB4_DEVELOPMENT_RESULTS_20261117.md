# R22 — Bd2 helps after …Bb4 and castling survives its removal

[Protocol](C3X_030_R22_BB4_DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL_20261117.md). Replayed the same legal histories from R21, with independent Berserk depth 12 and 14. All results repeated.

In the h4 branch, …Bb4 absolutely pins the c3 knight against the king. Bd2 interposes the white bishop and lifts that absolute pin. The legally verified exchange is Bd2 Bxc3 Bxc3 Qxc3+ bxc3. It supplies a concrete reply to the queen's participation in the line. Qd2 occupies d2 and prevents Bd2. O-O is also legal after h4 …Bb4 and removes the king from the pin line.

O-O was chosen at both depths when Bd2 was removed. The restriction changes the search context, so the three score columns are separate engine observations. The intervention supports a contribution from Bd2 and refutes a claim that it is necessary for h4's advantage. The …Ne7 control also lost 57 and 26 cp after Bd2 removal, while the …Qc7 control's values increased. The sensitivity is not confined to …Bb4.
