## 1. Certificate decomposition
Under the frozen P16 bridge grammar, a local minimal FULL_BRIDGE certificate at bound q requires a conjunction of increasingly outcome-bearing conditions. P10 separates these so that pre-intervention opportunity is not confused with causal realization.

### L0 — Pair/chain admissibility
The A/B pair and selected board chain survive the frozen P16 pair and chain support gates across the required engines. This is a design/support condition, not a causal result.

### L1 — Native semantic addressability
For bound q, a valid semantic event record exists in both B0 and TARGET:
`r0(q) != None AND rT(q) != None`.
This is the first opportunity condition. It can be measured with native CATALOG + fingerprint/addressability only.

### L2 — Native board preference reversal
The native roots on B0 and TARGET both lie in the A/B pair and differ:
`n0,nT in {A,B} AND n0 != nT`.
L2 is outcome-bearing and forbidden to the P10 opportunity probe.

### L3 — Event-removal collapse
Removing the matched q-event causes the B0 and TARGET roots to collapse to the same A/B move:
`t0,tT in {A,B} AND t0 == tT`.
This is direct intervention evidence.

### L4 — Sham exclusion
The same bridge pattern is not reproduced by SHAM. Otherwise the relation edit is not distinguished from matched collateral structure.

### L5 — Subset minimality
The same FULL_BRIDGE does not survive on SUBSET. This distinguishes MINIMAL_FULL_BRIDGE from a nonminimal bridge.

### L6 — Certificate materialization
A MINIMAL_FULL_BRIDGE record is converted into a local causal-contrast certificate under the frozen P16 authority ceiling.

### L7 — Structural replication
Only if the frozen exact/coarse replication requirements are met across witnesses, engines, positions, sources and physical edits may a local certificate become a replicated family member.

## 2. Logical implications
By construction:
`L7 => L6 => L5 => L4 => L3 => L2 => L1 => L0`.

The converses are not licensed. In particular, P10's opportunity state L1 is a necessary precondition for the corresponding bound-specific FULL_BRIDGE, but is not sufficient for native reversal, intervention collapse, sham exclusion, minimality or replication.

This is why P10 must not interpret high opportunity as causal evidence.

The most defensible representation is therefore:

No universal opportunity-transition law is earned.
