### Scientific finding 1: immediate capture has THREE alternative recapture refutations

At case #10 from original game FEN4 \`r3k2r/p1p5/1p2b1p1/4bp2/2P2N2/BP2PKP1/P7/3R1R2 b kq -\`, candidate \`...Bxf4\` (\`e5f4\`) takes a white knight (N=320 material units), but white has **three distinct legal immediate ways** to capture that bishop: \`e3f4\` (pawn), \`f3f4\` (king), \`g3f4\` (pawn). All three result in material delta **-10** for Black (N 320 minus bishop 330). Their independently estimated continuation scores are respectively -17cp, -8cp, -8cp at depth10. Meanwhile source actual \`...Kf7\` (\`e8f7\`) has minimum +45cp across all 37 legal White first replies at the same depth10-after-reply procedure; \`e3e4\` is one minimizer (ties possible). Therefore the immediate-gain description is not a valid unique *value* argument for \`...Bxf4\`, and this negative is robust to examining more than a single recapture. The actual original game followed \`...Kf7 e4 ...Bxf4 Kxf4\`; P32 unmodified depth18 root MultiPV also preferred \`Kf7\` +13cp to immediate \`Bxf4\` -13cp. These are concordant developmental observations, not a single-variable King-position causal intervention.

### Scientific finding 2: top3 can conceal the worst independently valued legal reply

At source case #14, foil root \`d5d3\`, P32 depth12 top3 after-root replies were \`c8c4\`, \`c8e6\`, \`g8h7\`. P33 independently searched **all 32 legally available** opponent replies and found \`b5b4\` had the lowest depth10-after-reply value (-13cp) among the whole fan, not appearing in that previous top3 list. This **does NOT prove depth12 MultiPV ranked incorrectly**, because depth10 forced-after-reply scores and depth12 after-root MultiPV values do not share exactly the same search horizon, search order or bounds. It **does** directly refute treating a depth12 top3 shortlist as an exhaustive legal-response court or a depth-independent certified worst response.

Also in #14 actual source played root \`c2c3\`: its actual human next move \`d8d7\` is NOT present in previous P32 top3, but is legally included among P33 all 28 and evaluated at +31cp vs newly found worst +25cp (\`e7f6\`); six centipawns higher is a depth10 estimate, not a human-response regret theorem.

### Scientific finding 3: relevant response discrimination is large even for simple actions

Case #6 actual \`c5c6\`: 34 legal opponent replies, forced minimum +91cp at \`b7a8\` (also the actual original response). Other candidate \`d3b5\`: 40 legal opponent replies, forced min +120cp at \`e8f8\`. The two root moves reach different legal reply domains and different resulting boards. These +91 and +120 figures cannot be interpreted as reliable same-run search-score differences without control for search horizon, root move grouping, and context. They show that a branch-aware method must condition on the root move and cannot naïvely pool reply sets across distinct chess positions.

Previous C3X 0.27 TT-FIRST work gives *physical engine-search causal evidence* of root TT perturbation; it does not show that the chess value of a visible bishop capture is the root mediator. The new contribution is a concrete controlled chess demonstration of **value-inferred contingent opponent counterplay** and the inadequacy of treating three selected replies as all possibilities. It is NOT a field-first theorem, NOT proof of a stable strategic explanation, and not superior to an equal-budget unmodified Stockfish oracle. In fact, the whole P33 calculation is still directly made from unmodified Stockfish values; any claim that full C3X explains more than Stockfish+PV or simple plan JSON must quantify extra explanatory content and test it against independent source labels, with node/time budgets matched.
