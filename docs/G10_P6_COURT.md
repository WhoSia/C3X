## Development result before holdout

The sealed four-certificate ecology already defeats three shortcuts:

- **Exact signature alone fails.** TWIC 1663 Berserk and Ethereal share the same P16 exact signature and board trigger but have different selected-bound response topology.
- **Response topology alone fails.** TWIC 1664 Ethereal and TWIC 1663 Berserk share the same normalized FULL_BRIDGE response topology while their board relation deltas differ.
- **Board relation delta alone fails.** The same TWIC 1663 pair shares its board trigger while response topology differs.

The frozen candidate constitutive core is therefore the conjunction:

`BOARD_RELATION_DELTA × SELECTED_BOUND_RESPONSE_TOPOLOGY`

Falsifier non-reproduction and chain-relative minimality remain authority obligations, not shortcuts for identity.
