# C3X G9.4-P26 — Architecture × budget product-space transport

## 1. Why P26 exists

P25 established two facts simultaneously: architecture identity remained necessary to index the observed intervention-response law family, and the fine K8 search-state atlas was materially search-budget dependent. P26 therefore stops treating search budget as a nuisance attached to an otherwise fixed state.

The candidate object is

`L(a,b,z_b,i) -> r`

with architecture `a`, node budget `b`, budget-constituted state `z_b`, source-level intervention `i`, and exact-world response `r`.

## 2. State correspondence is itself a scientific hypothesis

P26 does not assume that labels from different budgets denote the same state. It freezes a correspondence family

`tau_b : Z_b -> Z_80k`

before selective outcomes. The admissible capacity is deliberately tiny: an eight-state permutation chosen by maximum SHAM-cell overlap, with lexicographic tie-breaking. All cross-budget maps factor through the 80k anchor, so cycle consistency is built into the constitution.

This restriction follows the warning of Sutter et al. (2025): sufficiently expressive alignment maps can make causal abstraction vacuous. Failure under P26 therefore means failure under the frozen finite-permutation family, not under every possible nonlinear map.

Multi-Way Representation Alignment motivates the shared-universe factorization: pairwise maps are not independently optimized, and composition is constrained through one anchor.

## 3. Invariance before causality

Yao et al. (2025) show that latent-variable identification often depends on explicit invariance/equivalence relations rather than causal language alone. P26 therefore names the invariance relation mechanically: two budget-state labels are identified only through the frozen SHAM overlap permutation. The causal question comes later: after this equivalence relation is frozen, does intervention-response behavior commute across budgets and architectures?

## 4. Intervention validity

Grant et al. (2026) distinguish harmless from pernicious intervention-induced representational divergence. C3X source interventions do not synthesize arbitrary hidden activations; they suppress or alter admitted source-level memory semantics. That structural difference reduces, but does not eliminate, validity concerns. P26 therefore keeps intervention validity separate from state correspondence and never treats behavioral agreement alone as proof that an intervention stayed on the natural mechanism manifold.

## 5. Product-space removability

Architecture is removable only if every budget × mapped-state × relation-square cell has the same frozen response fingerprint across Stockfish 19, Berserk and Ethereal.

Budget is removable only if, within every architecture × mapped-state × square cell, the response fingerprint is identical across 40k, 80k, 160k and 300k nodes.

Only if both conditions hold does P26 grant product-space transport. Otherwise the smallest authorized object retains the failing index.

## 6. Equal-support relation geometry

Cross-budget remapping can change state support within material vertices. Relation geometry therefore cannot compare unequal empirical denominators. Before outcomes open, P26 freezes deterministic equalization: for each budget × mapped-state × square, candidate SHA orders each of the four material vertices, and all vertices are truncated to the minimum support. At least two rows per vertex are required.

## 7. Descriptor intervention feasibility

P25's descriptor map was observational. P26 separates observed descriptors from manipulable candidates.

- `probe_refreshes_age_on_hit`: source-local intervention candidate, pending semantic-equivalence preflight.
- `indexing_family`: topology-changing candidate; cannot be treated as a local toggle.
- `probe_returns_replacement_handle`: API/control-flow shape, not yet an admitted causal coordinate.
- `eval_move_packed_together`: layout-entangled and not admitted for causal manipulation.

Feasibility is not an effect estimate.

## 8. LawGen v2

LawGen v2 turns `state_correspondence`, `product_space`, `observed_descriptors`, `manipulable_descriptor_candidates`, and `prediction_authority` into explicit ontology objects. A successful compile says the experiment identity is internally executable; it does not say the causal field exists.

P26 is therefore a joint scientific and software test: the theory constrains the generator, and generator/CI failures reveal hidden assumptions before selective evidence is opened.
