## 1. Actually held and read original primary study

[Angelina Parfenova (2026), *Do Chess Explanations Reflect Model Decisions? Behavioral and Token-Level Tests of LLM Reasoning Faithfulness*, arXiv 2609.22245v1, Google Drive original](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pNg13f4_OII1N1GUThSwB8I5ru_3Ns_R/view).

Parfenova studies **200 Lichess endgame puzzles**, explanations generated by genuine external LLMs, two DISTINCT labels (**model's generated move** vs **gold reference puzzle solution**), true independent decoders of various strengths, strict/maximal masking, cross-puzzle random explanations, and open scorer model all-legal next-token likelihoods. The original claim that masked fluent text carries limited additional faithful move information is a finding with specific held-out/benchmark-model data.

R5 earlier produced **artificial templated explanations with explicit UCI and SAN strings**, across 4 previously exposed original chess root candidates in 2 historical chess games, each under 5 conditions. The synthetic text retains truthful ACT-Eval chess-tool facts such as capture/check even after UCI masking. R6 must not claim it reproduces Parfenova's original benchmark or its faithful-reasoning inference.

Second held original: [Hebbar et al. 2026 ACT-Eval in Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BOHr8mZzY6vp14PBr2MrAiJH9vJDQStA/view), whose deterministic factual verifier was implemented in R4 and used when composing R5 commentary.

## 3. R6-C semantic leakage falsification of “masked means no information”

Even if an LLM outputs no parseable UCI under masked text, the **actual synthetic masked explanation contains truthful statements about whether the nominated source move captures and/or checks**. Given FEN, enumerate all legal moves with the exact chess rules and filter these claims. This does **not** approximate source engine thinking; it directly tests whether residual masked text has *candidate-discriminating information*.

[Rule-based source-audit code](../harness/c3x_030_r6c_masked_semantic_information_audit.py) · [8 tests](../harness/test_c3x_030_r6c_masked_semantic_information_audit.py) · [**CI #42764157443 SUCCESS**](https://github.com/WhoSia/C3X/actions/runs/42764157443). The same exact frozen R5 truth audit SHA was verified.

| Exposed source action | Legal moves from full original state | Moves consistent with both masked factual predicates | Residual information from this fixed text, log2(N/k) bits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sahel `e5f4` | 43 | 41 | **0.0687127501** |
| Sahel `e5c3` | 43 | 41 | **0.0687127501** |
| Cerrado `e5f6` | 36 | **3** | **3.5849625007** |
| Cerrado `d1c2` | 36 | 33 | **0.1255308821** |

**Every one of the four masks retained some nonzero candidate discrimination from true capture/check facts; none alone uniquely identified the move in this small sample.** Under the source board for `e5f6`, a masked text narrowed 36 legal choices to 3 despite the Qwen 0.5B decoder having no parseable UCI output in that condition. This proves a representation/protocol gap: lexical masking and generation decoding failure do NOT imply no residual semantic information; whether a capable decoder recovers that information requires a separate empirical test. It also suggests checking whether the original Parfenova "maximal" mask would remove or retain these facts; **we cannot relabel this approximate regex as the original maximal masking**.

Formal counting under uniform legal-action prior: `I=log2(N/k)`, e.g. `log2(36/3)=log2(12)≈3.585` bits for `e5f6`. Not mutual information of a population, not calibrated uncertainty of the actual model, nor causal chess evaluation. No new Stockfish/Berserk native searches.
