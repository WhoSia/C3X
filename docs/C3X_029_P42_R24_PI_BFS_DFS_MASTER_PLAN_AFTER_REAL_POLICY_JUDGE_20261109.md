## I. The real target is a joint causal–semantic proposition

For an original board with full legal game history (s), unrestricted source engine (E), actual root move (m_E(s)), pre-judge frozen adversarial policy (\pi_E(s,m,r)) and independent referee family (J), the scientific question is:

> Which physically executed source computation **causes** the original engine to favor (m_E(s)) over a real alternative, and does the affected computation faithfully encode a prospective strategically meaningful future property (H(s,m,r,\pi)) that can survive diverse legal opponent responses and an independent chess-value test?

Neither of these halves implies the other:
- Finding any source statement whose perturbation flips a root **does not** make it a chess-strategy explanation. R18–R20 show one exact cycle-alpha source event changes intermediate legal root choices (depth 9–11), but not final Game14 action; the global R9/R10 draw function does affect final choice, yet the local unique mediation remains unidentified.
- Finding a good-looking plan or low per-root regret **does not** prove it motivated the source engine's actual natural root choice. Pure within-root regret is mathematically invariant to a move-specific offset of all its leaves (P42/R1).
- Judging the source plan with a separate finite-depth engine is *not* an exact oracle. R24/B2 has 55 negative "unrestricted minus forced" finite-search signed gaps even though one imagines unrestricted optima should dominate a restricted move. Different search budgets/trees make the numerical oracle identity false.
- Good B2 values on **post-R22-selected Sahel/Cerrado** do NOT attach to mechanistic **September Game14**, or P34 NNUE static boards. A same-position verified JOIN is missing.

One credible flagship theorem-like **empirical claim**, after future work, could be: *On a predeclared, genuinely novel disjoint cohort of chess positions, a source-exact mediator whose perturbation changes the original unrestricted root choice also changes a frozen future-plan property that is valuable under independent adversarial legal continuations, with calibrated counterfactual uncertainty, strong baselines and at least one disconfirming mechanism test.* Without the JOIN, the claim is **HOLD**.

## IV. Explicit evidentiary join contract

A future paper claim must contain one reproducible **same world** receipt:
1. Source original full legal game history and event ID from a **before-outcome frozen, disjoint** cohort, canonical byte/hash comparison (not mismatched hash algorithms).
2. All source legal first-root candidates and predeclared contrast; candidate set cannot disappear when a third legal root appears.
3. Actual native unrestricted root decision with identical resources/sham, precise source computational perturbation, TT/ancestor/window/research search support and cold provenance.
4. Pre-judge `\pi(r)` frozen for all legal opponent first replies (and selective own multi-step partial tree at k=3), no arbitrary advantage claims.
5. Independent second engine-family referee evaluates the same actions/histories/response policies with confidence and cost reporting; finite-depth judge uncertainty and positive/negative signed gap anomalies predeclared.
6. Strong published Cui/BinProp/Queen-style comparison as appropriate, fair within specified cost frontier and abstention; external chess player utility only when actually measured.
7. Full prior exposure registry and new event-disjoint prospective replay. If missing, report an explanatory mechanism *development result*, not a general causal–strategic chess explanation.
