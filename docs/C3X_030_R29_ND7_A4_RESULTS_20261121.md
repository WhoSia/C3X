# C3X 0.30 R29 — Where the two local TT effects stop

Fixture `twic1670-08-61`, identical 61-ply history, all 34 legal roots,
Stockfish 16 NNUE, one thread, 16 MiB hash and 32,000 requested nodes.
For each intervention the original executable, instrumented trace-off
and discovery outputs matched. Sham, repeated mask and restoration
were fresh processes. Each experiment matched the identity of the
physical probe before intervening and withheld one local read.

| Nominated read | Control | Withheld | Endpoint |

| Probe 557, attempt 26, …a4 child | b4 hint; searches b4 then bxa4 first | bxa4 then b4 first | Native child +666 and root −666 both times. …a4 keeps the same leader status in attempt 26. Final …Nd7 survives, but depth 14/15 producer and public point change from −257cp to −242cp. |
| Probe 909, attempt 37, …a4 child | bxa4 hint; stored +885 lower bound meets child beta +845 and exits before looking at a reply | bxa4 hint remains; no stored-value cutoff; searches bxa4 | Child +885 and root −885 in both arms. …Nd7 remains leader at −845 in the same attempt, and both final outputs recommend …Nd7 at −257cp. Reported nodes 32,003 versus 32,005. |

No successful b4 hint was observed in attempt 37. The predeclared
fallback picked probe 557 from attempt 26. The later bound test was a
follow-up after reading the first baseline trace, frozen before running
the bound intervention. It used the genuine logged cutoff in the producing
attempt because that was the observed return path. The child-to-root sign
negation and same root-candidate ID were logged. The −885 value at
attempt 37 is below root alpha and cannot be subtracted from the
leader's −845 as an exact candidate-score gap.

The two masks had measurable effects. The early move ordering
intervention changed the later public score and producing iteration but
did not dislodge the final choice. The producing-attempt value
intervention established that the recorded TT cutoff was one sufficient
way to obtain the observed upper-bound comparison. Searching the
position again in the same window yielded the same return. It left the
ranking relative to the leader and the final move intact.

Separately, R26's independent Berserk enumerated White's replies.
After …a4, 32.b4 was the minimum at both tested leaf depths. The black
margins in favor of …Nd7 were +83/+96cp across all replies and
+54/+25cp when the scored b4 branch was omitted. The white b-pawn
protects c5 in legal replay. This still supplies a board explanation
with its separate reference regime. The producing-attempt native trace
used a cached bound with a bxa4 hint, and the follow-up without that
bound searched bxa4. The R29 observations do not establish mediation
from b4 to Stockfish's actual choice.
