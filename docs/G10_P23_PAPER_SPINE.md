# C3X G10-P23 — Paper Spine (Pre-Outcome)

## Working scientific thesis

C3X studies marginal engine preference between near-equal legal moves as a causal, contrastive object rather than treating an engine score or internal trace as self-explanatory.

G10-P23 introduces **executable intervention support** as a separate prospective object from mechanism activation:

- world availability does not imply pair activity;
- pair activity does not imply routed-chain realizability;
- routed-chain realizability does not imply mediator-class activation;
- mediator-class activation does not imply a specific Q0 fiber mechanism.

Let

[
R(e,x)=1
]

iff engine (e), world (x), and the frozen contrast grammar admit a deterministic legal and repeatably measurable TARGET/SUBSET/SHAM intervention before mediator-class outcomes are opened.

Let

[
A_f(e,x)=mathbf 1{S^{mathrm{FULL_CLASS}_f}_e(x)
eq S^{mathrm{BASE}}_e(x)}.
]

P23's primary transport question is therefore conditional and explicit:

[
P(A_fmid R=1,	ext{pre-intervention BASE features}),
]

not population-wide activation over worlds where the intervention is undefined.

## Candidate novelty

The narrow claim to defend is:

> **Executable intervention support can be prospectively separated from mediator activation and used as an outcome-blind acquisition constitution for external validation of causal mechanism predictions.**

This is deliberately narrower than claiming a new general theory of causal transportability. Standard transportability, positivity/overlap, and two-phase sampling are methodological donors. C3X's proposed contribution is the chess-specific evidence class in which **intervention realizability itself is measured, sealed, and used before treatment-response outcomes are opened**.

## Why P23 is not just a sampling fix

P22 showed that ordinary geometry/pair qualification can leave the final causal experiment under-supported after routed-chain construction.

P23 changes the scientific object and the experiment order:

[
	ext{fresh world pool}
	o
	ext{pair qualification}
	o
	ext{executable routed-chain support }R
	o
	ext{support seal}
	o
	ext{activation prediction seal}
	o
	ext{FULL_CLASS outcome}.
]

The acquisition rule may condition on (R), but may not condition on predicted or observed (A_f).

This makes support failure and model failure separately observable.

## Live rivals

### H_SUPPORT_ONLY

P22's apparent transport problem was support insufficiency. Once (R=1) quotas are prospectively guaranteed, the frozen P22 R1 activation field transports.

### H_SUPPORT_PLUS_SHIFT

Executable support is necessary but not sufficient. Ecological shift changes activation calibration even after (R=1) is guaranteed.

### H_FIELD_FAILURE

Adequate (R=1) support exists, but the frozen activation field fails the preregistered prospective thresholds.

## Frozen P22 field

The development-selected field is R1_BOUNDARY_PLUS_LOAD.

Decision rule:

1. if `base_max_gap_cp > 74.5` → INACTIVE;
2. if `base_max_gap_cp <= 30.5`, ACTIVE iff `candidate_side_event_imbalance > 0.19`;
3. if `30.5 < base_max_gap_cp <= 74.5`, ACTIVE iff `family_event_count_target > 1926.5`.

It may not be refit before P23 primary adjudication.

## Primary P23 thresholds

After the executable-support seal:

- balanced accuracy ≥ 0.70;
- positive recall ≥ 0.50;
- negative specificity ≥ 0.70;
- at least one positive held-out activation;
- source robustness: balanced accuracy ≥ 0.60 in at least two held-out source ecologies containing both classes.

Support prerequisites are separately frozen:

- 3 sources;
- ≥2 ecology types;
- ≥18 routed-chain-realizable positions;
- ≥54 engine×world×family support cells;
- ≥12 support cells/source;
- ≥2 engines;
- source support ratio ≤2.0.

## Selection-bias firewall

The primary threat is that conditioning on executable support can silently become conditioning on activation.

P23 therefore forbids before support seal:

- FULL_CLASS response;
- activation labels;
- frozen R1 ACTIVE/INACTIVE predictions;
- Q0 intervention outcomes;
- post-intervention scores/PVs.

The support-conditioned target population must be stated explicitly. P23 does **not** claim that the resulting sample represents all chess positions.

## Track A / Track B separation

### Track A — scientific authority

Authority-bearing objects include:

- P23 constitution and acquisition preseal;
- executable-support receipts;
- prediction seal;
- FULL_CLASS outcome artifacts;
- prospective adjudication.

A Track A PASS can authorize a bounded causal route only inside its recorded scope.

### Track B — implementation authority

The PGN explanation product consumes the support state but cannot create scientific authority.

Machine-readable states:

- `SUPPORTED`;
- `ABSTAIN_CHAIN_REALIZABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`;
- `ABSTAIN_PARENT_CLASS_INACTIVE`;
- `ABSTAIN_ACTIVATION_SCOPE_UNCERTAIN`.

A causal certificate is surfaceable only when any applicable pre-causal authority route permits it.

This creates a practical product consequence from the scientific constitution without treating product quality as evidence for the science.

### Support passes; field passes

## Manuscript architecture

1. **Problem** — marginal engine preference is not explained by score, trace, or mechanism catalogue alone.
2. **Failure sequence** — P19 scalar ordering → P20 heterogeneous class mediation → P21 local fiber mediation but fresh parent extinction → P22 activation-field development but validation support collapse.
3. **Methodological distinction** — availability, pair activity, executable support, class activation, local mediator.
4. **P23 design** — outcome-blind support-constrained acquisition.
5. **Prospective transport test** — frozen P22 field on a support-sealed fresh population.
6. **Two-track system** — the same support state becomes a causal-language authority firewall in the PGN explainer.
7. **Limitations** — conditional target population, engine/query/protocol dependence, no human-cognition claim.
8. **Generalization hypothesis** — executable-support conditioning may be useful for other mechanistic explanation systems, but cross-domain transfer is not claimed here.

## Claims to avoid

- “first ever” or broad causal-inference novelty without a dedicated interdisciplinary literature review;
- population-wide chess activation effects;
- universal numerical thresholds;
- human strategic intent or cognition;
- objective chess truth;
- treating Track B CI/utility as Track A validation;
- treating (R) as a causal mechanism rather than an experimental support condition.

## Publication decision after P23

A manuscript becomes defensible even under a prospective FAIL if:

1. the support seal passes without activation conditioning;
2. the frozen field is prospectively adjudicated on adequate support;
3. implementation demonstrates machine-readable authority routing without scientific leakage;
4. the manuscript states the conditional estimand and selection limitation explicitly.

A PASS strengthens the transport claim. A FAIL strengthens the support-vs-model separation result. A support HOLD remains methodologically informative but would likely require another fresh prospective validation stage before a full empirical paper.
