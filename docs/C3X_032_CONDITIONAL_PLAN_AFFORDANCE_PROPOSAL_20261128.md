## New estimand
For legal history h and candidate pair A,B, compare a *conditional plan profile*: for each candidate, legal opponent replies, continuation policy, horizon, independently specified board/plan predicates, and uncertainty status. The target is whether a named contrast remains true and decision-relevant under a preregistered reply ecology and judge, not simply whether one PV exhibits a favorable motif.
Represent separate candidate-specific reply sets (do not equate illegal or semantically different moves). Report reply coverage, value ranges, plan survival and counterexamples separately. Only call a robust preference within the stated envelope if the direction survives all required reference settings and reply policies; otherwise present conditional comparison/abstention, not equality. A shared endpoint with the same legal state and history semantics is a transposition control, not proof that all earlier choices are equivalent.
