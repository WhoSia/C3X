Input: preselected disjoint independent games; at each selected board, two near-equal legal moves a,b from calibrated root-restricted budget, plus optional third legal competitor; record uncertainty and stop/abstain if unstable.
Competing models: H1 score/rank-only explanation, H2 generic concept label difference, H3 action-conditioned explanation with legally verifiable opponent-reply counterfactual.
Preseal independent chess consequence claims (threat, tactic, pawn structure, king safety, legal reply responses) and a prediction of which candidate/condition falsifies the explanation; evaluate with chess rules and independent oracle/protocol. Outcomes: structural truth/falsification rate, ungrounded-claim rate, coverage/abstention, effect of reply perturbation, stability over depth and source ecology, optional human comprehension study with blind scoring. Report confidence by GAME, not by correlated arms.
Design negative: H3 may fail; a failed explanation is a result, not permission to invent a more attractive motif after seeing answers.
