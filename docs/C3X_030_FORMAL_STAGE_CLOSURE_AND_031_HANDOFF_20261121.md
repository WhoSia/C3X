# C3X 0.30 — Research-Phase Closure Court, Historical Non-Erasure & 0.31 Question Migration

**Date:** 2026-11-21. **Decision:** **CLOSE C3X 0.30 AS A RESEARCH PHASE WITH SCIENTIFIC HOLD TRANSFER.** This is **NOT** a certificate that the founding Stockfish near-equal-move explanation problem, full internal mediation, strategy generalization, NNUE model causality or learner outcomes have been solved. **C3X 0.31 is PROPOSED, not automatically activated.**

## 1. Why a version boundary is legitimate without a false PASS

C3X 0.30 began with D1: physically identify whether native Stockfish source events causally influence ancestor/root move selection rather than proliferate commentary methods. In this version, a meaningful research question has now been answered within bounded engine contexts: **native TT/search-state interventions can alter a downstream root choice; the publicly visible score/PV may fail to expose such internal changes, and intermediate state/finite-budget effects prevent a unique scalar-score explanation.**

That is not the same as proving that the chosen move is better chess or that our explanation improves learning. Flowing Versioning permits moving the **primitive research target**, while preserving precisely those unresolved claims. This closure is a question-migration record, not a scientific promotion of unobserved quantities.

### R37's decisive counterexample to a false strategic conclusion

R30-017's source intervention changed Stockfish's final recommendation from **Qf3** to **Qd3**, the latter missing from the original independent judge support. The new original-source-pinned Berserk 14 referee evaluated *every* legal Black first reply after each of Qf3, Qd3 and a3. White-centric minimum finite cp at depths 10/12 was:

- **Qf3:** 74 / 72.
- **Qd3:** 62 / 60.
- **a3:** 53 / 49.

The bounded worst-reply order was Qf3 > Qd3 > a3 at both depths. Furthermore, against the **same 45 Black replies**, Qf3's value exceeded Qd3 for 33/45 (depth10) and 34/45 (depth12), but Qd3 exceeded Qf3 for 11/45 and 10/45, respectively, with one tie at each depth. Thus there is neither an independent Qd3-over-Qf3 overall ranking nor Qf3 universal response-by-response dominance under those finite observations.

The chess result therefore blocks both overclaims: “source-induced final move switch implies superior objective strategy” and “the numerical worst-reply ranking implies superiority under every legal response.” The scientific object has become a **conditional response policy and the decision state that predicts source choice**, not one magic evaluation difference.

## 3. What is settled and what is NOT

**Admitted within recorded scope:**
1. Native source state/search events can be physically intervened and the finite search's final recommended move can change.
2. The public depth/score/bound/PV/final recommendation projection is **not injective** with respect to the measured source-event returns in recorded pairs.
3. Changes in local candidate scoring can fail to reach final bestmove; different upstream events can converge on a later root competition difference.
4. Finite-depth independent legal reply evaluation can diverge from the preference produced by a separate intervened source search.
5. The stored technical evidence, genuine negative results and historical naming must remain available.

**HOLD / transferred:**
- **H1 mediator:** Show the actual *intermediate* TT-value/depth/bound, history/order and budget-state chain from the physical event to a final move, using a valid same-prefix checkpoint and necessary/sufficient restoration. R35 observes downstream matched-window behavior but not exclusive mediation.
- **H2 transport:** Learn a minimal state representation on developer histories and falsify/test it on independently sourced game/event-disjoint positions; preserve source and sham controls, no post-outcome sample switching.
- **H3 future chess value:** Repeat conditional all-legal-reply planning on original full legal game history, two independent engine depths or engine families and actual three-plus candidate sets. R37 is FEN-only one-case development evidence and finite, not an exact oracle.
- **H4 human transfer:** Collect ethically appropriate blinded chess-learning outcomes with original R31 4-arm protocol and truly unseen position/motif families. Existing four-arm learner **materials**, not participants or effect sizes, are completed.
- **H5 NNUE representation:** Tie original NNUE representations by source interventions to actual root choice and legal future chess concepts rather than label static activations post hoc.
- **H6 peer-review and literature:** Before novelty/paper claims, compare original owned primary PDFs and properly run strong published baselines at equivalent evidence/budget. No implied superiority to Cui, SARFA, Queen, BinProp etc.

A failed or unsupported future gate is to be retained as data, not converted to a local-only PASS by shrinking denominators. R30-017 already illustrates why a single finite source-causal effect is not a strategic benefit claim.
