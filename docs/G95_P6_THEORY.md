# C3X 0.7.0-G9.5-P6 — Counterexample-Sequestered Relational Causal Representation Reconstitution

## Scientific question

P5 established a sharp failure mode: a bounded strict-prefix Cartesian representation grammar can produce zero-collision train partitions while every surviving representation fails a fresh schema-selection split. P6 does not patch those seven representations. It asks whether a **prospectively frozen relational representation language**, with tightly bounded interaction capacity, can recover train-to-selection stability and then survive untouched cross-engine transport.

The P5 Ethereal selection counterexample is **development-only**. It may motivate the move from flat coordinates to relations, but its label receives zero P6 confirmatory vote. P6 never downloads P5 selection-target artifacts.

## Strict-prefix relational object

P6 keeps `STRICT_EVENT_PREFIX_ONLY`. For each candidate event, the observable object is a typed local prefix graph whose current-event node may relate only to already-observed parent-trace nodes.

Permitted relation families are fixed before P6 target opening:

- immediate predecessor relation;
- previous same-key relation;
- previous same-class relation;
- same-key lineage length/diversity;
- normalized three-event equality motifs;
- previous-same-key move/depth/ply transitions.

No raw transposition-table key is emitted. Equality and recurrence may be used; key identity may not.

## Why this is not arbitrary non-linearity

P6 does not permit an unrestricted nonlinear map. The representation language is finite and auditable:

1. a frozen vocabulary of relational atoms;
2. a small frozen set of ordinary strict-prefix context atoms;
3. a frozen set of named two-factor interaction templates;
4. at most three basis terms in a representation;
5. at most one interaction term;
6. an explicit description-length cost per term plus a cell cost.

This directly targets the vacuity risk exposed by increasingly expressive representation maps: representational accuracy is scientifically useful only when the representation class and complexity budget are constrained before outcomes.

## Fresh nested sequestration

P6 uses three new disjoint role sets selected from the pinned official-stockfish/books snapshot after excluding all recoverable P1–P5 candidate hashes.

1. **RELATIONAL_TRAIN** — targets may be opened for bounded representation learning.
2. **RELATIONAL_SELECTION** — candidate predictions are sealed before selection targets are opened; no refit.
3. **UNTOUCHED_TRANSPORT** — the selected field is scoped before transport targets may be opened.

The P5 shared counterexample and all P5 selection labels are forbidden from all three roles.

## Relational basis

Every primary field retains the F0 `fiber_id` anchor.

Atomic relation terms describe prefix relations such as same-key recurrence state, same-key depth direction, current/previous key equality, normalized local key/class motifs, and key-lineage class/scope diversity.

A small ordinary context set remains available for grounding relational terms in event/board state.

Interaction terms are not freely generated. Only prospectively named templates are allowed, such as:

- same-key depth direction × event-window relation;
- same-key class relation × TT-bound category;
- key-lineage length × move presence;
- local key motif × event-window relation;
- board development × same-key depth direction.

## Complexity ceiling

Candidate representation:

```
fiber_id × term_1 × ... × term_k,  1 <= k <= 3
```

with at most one interaction term.

Description cost:

```
L_desc = sum(term_cost_bits)
L_total = L_desc + number_of_distinct_train_cells
```

Atomic relation terms cost 10 bits, ordinary context atoms 10 bits, and predeclared interactions 18 bits. A candidate exceeding the frozen description ceiling is inadmissible.

Train admissibility additionally requires zero train collisions, compression, cross-position reuse, cross-engine reuse, both labels, and repeated positive support inside at least one reused cell. That final condition is explicitly intended to reject singleton-positive memorisation.

## Selection support before selection judgment

P5 selection contained only one positive. P6 therefore separates **selection support adequacy** from representation validity.

Selection verdicts are interpreted only if the fresh selection split clears its own frozen support gate. If selection support is inadequate, P6 closes at `SELECTION_SUPPORT_HOLD`; it does not call a representation stable or unstable.

No threshold changes are allowed after selection targets open.

## Selection and transport

A representation passes fresh selection only with zero covered contradictions, zero mixed observed cells, broad cross-engine/source/position coverage, and at least two covered positives spanning at least two source regimes.

Only a selection-passing representation may scope untouched transport. Transport targets open only if the target-blind scope gate is met. Public certification requires exact agreement on every covered transport profile.

## Claim ceiling

A P6 PASS would establish that one bounded relational representation family survived prospective train, selection and untouched cross-engine transport under this exact-event replay regime.

It would not prove that the representation is unique, that its terms are physical mechanisms, that the same law holds in all chess engines, or that it describes human chess cognition.
