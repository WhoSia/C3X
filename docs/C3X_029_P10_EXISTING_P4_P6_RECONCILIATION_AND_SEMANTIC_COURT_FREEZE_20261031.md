## Research frontier now: "short executable chess explanation versus strong common-evidence baseline"
Question: Given matched legal near-equivalent alternatives and unchanged evidence, can a small predeclared feature-limited CONTINGENT opponent-response explanation (P5/P6) correctly predict response-dependent chess consequences that ordinary generic labels and score/PV narratives miss on independent heldout games?

Adversarial rivals:

R1 common human-style chess labels using the SAME allowed source evidence
R2 C3X typed contingent certificate with explicit domain, witness for conjunction, and opponent refuters; may abstain.

Test design without outcome contamination:
S0 Prepare fully isolated, source-disjoint and game-clustered pool; no May2, September14, Olympiad8, archived P3 indices42/43/47/99 in confirmatory group.
S1 Prior to exposure to candidate labels, freeze candidate pair selection threshold from repeated equal-budget root restricted evaluation and quantify disagreement/instability (unresolved positions retained as ABSTAIN; forbid assuming near-equality from one noisy cp). Use exact state including game history.
S2 Freeze 2–3 finite-horizon legal predicates and their syntactic meaning BEFORE scoring; distinguish all-legal-replies from capture-only domains; no empirical probability inference from response count.
S3 Primary outcome: independently replayable legal counterexample rate and fraction of emitted comparative claims with valid SAME-WITNESS contingent certificate; compare R0/R1/R2 at equal board/provenance under game-level clustering. Secondary: refusal/coverage, explanation length, robustness across depth/budget and, when separately recruited and approved, blinded human understanding.
S4 Hard refutation: one legal opponent counterexample can falsify a universal claim; absence within censored top-k cannot certify one. P and Q separately true with disjoint response witnesses -> explicit FALSE for conjunction.
