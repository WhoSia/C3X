# C3X Semantic Version Boundary — 0.8.0 → 0.9.0

## Binding boundary
- `C3X 0.8.0-G10-P14` is the terminal checkpoint of the 0.8.0 line.
- `C3X 0.9.0-G10-P15` is the opening checkpoint of the 0.9.0 line.
- Historical stage names are immutable and are not retroactively renumbered.
- The Generation X scientific P lineage continues without reset: P14 → P15.

## Version semantics
- Minor bump `0.x.0`: the scientific architecture, ontology, or intervention grammar changes materially.
- Patch bump `0.x.y`: implementation, instrumentation, protocol, or bounded repair inside the same scientific architecture.
- `G#`: long-horizon research generation.
- `P#`: scientific mainline question inside that generation.

## Why 0.9.0
P14 established that balanced chess-state acquisition and near-equal A/B pair acquisition are feasible, while the inherited P16 relation-preserving intervention grammar is the dominant bottleneck. P15 therefore changes the intervention grammar itself rather than merely executing another experiment under the 0.8.0 architecture.

This is a semantic architecture change, not a patch.
