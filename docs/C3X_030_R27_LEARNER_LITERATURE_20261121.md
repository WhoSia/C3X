# C3X R27 — Prior work and what changes in the experiment

## Closest chess work

- **Cui, Ling & Ng (2026), §§6.3 and D.** Rechecked the [Drive PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19zqCJXM631qasR9uIyg4GuBZxIBO7czw/view) against the [ACL published study description and Table 2](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1560/). Thirty students played 30 puzzles through a pruned set of defender branches. The verbalized strategy remained visible, enabling a test of assisted play. In Table 2 the human aggregate scores are .437 without strategy, .531 with the human text and .513 with the LLM text at evaluation width three. The first ten puzzles used no strategy. R27 retains active moves on off-PV replies and gives all study arms the same items in the eventual randomized experiment. It adds an unassisted test on a different game after the study material has been closed. It treats assistance, near transfer and durable learning as separate outcomes.
- **Press & Golubev (2026), BinProp.** Read the [public text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23166v1), especially its paired continuations and deterministic board predicates. A board predicate can recur in both candidate lines and fail to distinguish them. For C3X, encode the proposed lesson as a checkable action with explicit exceptions, such as whether a nominated pawn can advance and legally recapture a knight. Include positions where the motif fails. This gives a reference answer for a defined board task. It supplies no teacher ranking for the entire position. [Drive listing](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gfmfug6814iYSEN3CkSF1xcsiG9uGTx6/view); the raw read of this copy did not complete in this round.
- **Schut et al. (2025).** The [PNAS study](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2406675122) filters AlphaZero concepts for teachability and novelty, then reports a preliminary exercise with four grandmasters on prototype positions. This makes AI-to-human transfer an existing research direction. C3X asks a different empirical question about comparative advice for a named Stockfish choice and learners outside that elite sample. Keep conditions, prototype items and evaluation items distinct. [Drive listing](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Mg4vfb2t-MIr6juCAzCC1qdv8sq1f8uj/view), [Oxford](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A57766c04-fe72-43f7-966a-132dfaaf27d7).
- **Hebbar et al. (2026), ACT-Eval.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04240) prescribes separately checking atomic chess facts, concept coverage and judgments of move quality. In R27, legal geometry and answer moves are validated before any question reaches a learner. If two independent search depths disagree about move quality, record the disagreement and give that item an audit outcome.

## Outside chess

| Study and experimental lesson | Import to C3X |

| Hase & Bansal (2020), [ACL](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.491/) · [Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GXj08LYZjYGKY6QiYlyo4JFnc0HBGQ0M/view). Separate explained examples from test examples, measure predictions of the model under changes, and record whether it agrees with an external reference. Reported preferences did not reliably identify useful explanations. | Separate prediction of Stockfish output from choosing a sound chess move. Include genuine engine/reference disagreement for trust calibration. |
| Bansal et al. (2021), [CHI](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/does-the-whole-exceed-its-parts-the-effect-of-ai-explanations-on-complementary-team-performance/) · [Drive preprint](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1REWt7ZM5q4e6coHQxz_ylttG-ji9xa6G/view). In these tasks, explanations increased acceptance even on erroneous recommendations. | Compare acceptance after a source-backed report on concordant versus discordant examples. A true description of the source process can accompany an unreliable recommendation. |
| Buçinca, Malaya & Gajos (2021), [CSCW](https://doi.org/10.1145/3449287) · [Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zhxm2rQiepYaI_p-Y6wc7AUD8OqarABQ/view). Cognitive forcing reduced overreliance in their experiment, with a satisfaction trade-off and heterogeneous benefits. | Ask for a candidate move before revealing the lesson in all three conditions. Evaluate whether the first-attempt prompt helps in its own later treatment so the native evidence contrast remains interpretable. |
| Chi et al. (1989), [mechanics worked examples](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1); Renkl et al. (2002), [faded examples](https://escholarship.org/content/qt81b9j9hs/qt81b9j9hs_noSplash_ef3e30a960524adc5157ece2d6fa3130.pdf). Learners articulated the conditions under which worked steps apply. Fading steps was tested for transfer, with clearer evidence for near transfer. | Remove the worked line at test. A possible later progression is a complete example, an unshown reply to the example, then a new game requiring the same reasoning. A correct answer requires a legal action, not simply recognizing a label. |
| Jacobs et al. (2021), [clinician-vignette experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7862671/), Table 3. Presentation of incorrect machine recommendations was associated with worse choices under both the overall comparison and the feature-based-versus-baseline comparison. | Include precisely identified unsupported or contradicted advice in a later audit block. Measure excessive uptake separately from justified reliance. This transfers a study design, with no assumed chess effect. |
| Miller (2019), [social-science review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2018.07.007). Explanations typically address a contrast and select a few relevant causes. | Allow the player to name the competing move. Select an observed branch that changes the comparison and make underlying engine provenance available for inspection. |
