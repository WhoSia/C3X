# C3X 0.8.0-G10-P1 — Explanation-Demand Validity Court

**Formal stage:** C3X 0.8.0-G10-P1 — Prospective Explanation-Demand Validity, Opening-Theory Degeneracy Exclusion, Human↔Engine Candidate-Salience Triangulation, Cross-Engine/Budget Stability, Decision-Pressure Stratification, Annotation-Supported Positive-Control Recovery, Frozen Real-PGN Case-Bank Constitution & Fresh Local-Certificate Induction Admission Court

## Scientific purpose

P0 proved that C3X can run a real-PGN → question → local-certificate → chess-native consequence → commentary loop without authority leakage. It did **not** establish that the P0 demand selector identifies the right questions. P1 therefore spends evidence on the selector itself before any fresh local-certificate outcome is opened.

## Frozen rival ecology

- **R0:** P0's near-equal candidate rule is already a useful demand class.
- **R1:** opening theory creates false-positive demand because multiple standard continuations are naturally near-equal.
- **R2:** rating-conditioned human move distributions reveal a distinct human-salience geometry.
- **R3:** candidate-pair identity is unstable across engines or budgets.
- **R4:** decision pressure/ambiguity creates meaningful strata but is not itself an exclusion rule.
- **R5:** expert human annotation should provide an external attention control if the selector is measuring something explanation-relevant.

## Evidence firewall

P1 is prohibited from reading or generating fresh local-certificate intervention outcomes. No certificate ID, causal family, structural signature, intervention result, replication status or certificate yield can enter selection.

Annotation labels are **external evaluation only**. They never tune P1 gates.

## Opening-theory axis

Use `lichess-org/chess-openings@c67912be581f0793dbaa776be5ccf111e01f88d9`. Convert its PGN lines into UCI prefixes and classify every position as exact named position, inside a known line, immediately past a named line, or no named prefix. No ply-only opening classifier is admissible for degeneracy exclusion.

A case is prospectively marked **opening-theory-degenerate** only when it is close to a known opening line **and** Maia-3 says both engine candidates have nontrivial human-policy support. This avoids declaring every named opening position uninteresting.

## Human↔engine salience

Primary instrument: Maia-3 5M, code `1e13597c42d4858b7cfd7cfdae01e297263364b2`, checkpoint snapshot frozen before scoring.

Frozen rating bands: 1200 / 1800 / 2400.

For each band record:
- played-move probability/rank;
- both engine-candidate probabilities/ranks;
- pair probability mass;
- top-k mass and normalized entropy;
- top-1 identity.

A pair is human-supported only if both candidate moves receive at least 0.03 probability and joint mass at least 0.20 in at least two rating bands.

## Verdict rules

- Maia failure → `HOLD_HUMAN_SALIENCE_INSTRUMENT_UNREALIZED`.
- Missing engine/opening/annotation instrument, final bank <18, fewer than two phases, or no annotation enrichment ≥1.05 → `HOLD_DEMAND_SELECTOR_EXTERNAL_VALIDITY_UNRESOLVED`.
- Otherwise, if ≥25% of the base bank is filtered or opening-degenerate/engine-unstable share is ≥20% → `PASS_WITH_SELECTOR_SHRINKAGE_CASE_BANK_FROZEN`.
- Otherwise → `PASS_DEMAND_VALIDITY_CASE_BANK_FROZEN`.

No P1 verdict authorizes causal claims.
