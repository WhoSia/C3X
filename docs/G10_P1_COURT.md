## Scientific purpose

P0 proved that C3X can run a real-PGN → question → local-certificate → chess-native consequence → commentary loop without authority leakage. It did **not** establish that the P0 demand selector identifies the right questions. P1 therefore spends evidence on the selector itself before any fresh local-certificate outcome is opened.

## Frozen rival ecology

- **R0:** P0's near-equal candidate rule is already a useful demand class.
- **R1:** opening theory creates false-positive demand because multiple standard continuations are naturally near-equal.
- **R2:** rating-conditioned human move distributions reveal a distinct human-salience geometry.
- **R3:** candidate-pair identity is unstable across engines or budgets.
- **R4:** decision pressure/ambiguity creates meaningful strata but is not itself an exclusion rule.
- **R5:** expert human annotation should provide an external attention control if the selector is measuring something explanation-relevant.
