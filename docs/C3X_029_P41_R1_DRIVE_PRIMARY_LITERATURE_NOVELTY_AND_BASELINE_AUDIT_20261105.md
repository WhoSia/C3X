## First-order closest rival: Cui, Ling & Ng (2026)
Drive original PDF `19zqCJXM631qasR9uIyg4GuBZxIBO7czw`, [official ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1560/), *Communicating Chess Strategies in Natural Language* (Findings EMNLP 2026). Their strategy is a partial mapping from chess states to actions, built as a **partial engine strategy tree** using up to `k_strat=3` top engine-ranked opponent options. It is serialized to JSON and/or natural language. Evaluation uses **Tree-Expanded Puzzle Playing**: sample `k_eval=3` opponent actions, query actual solving player for response, evaluate the resulting board with **Stockfish 16**, and continue the most unfavorable sampled branch. 100 Lichess puzzles, five runs, and a **30-university-student human study**. They explicitly claim off-main-line evaluation, strategic pedagogical utility, and acknowledge player-specific confounding, human/LLM preference mismatch, and omitted naturalness/intelligibility dimensions. Their empirical result: language guidance can improve over no guidance but often loses information relative to direct JSON strategy. **C3X cannot claim first off-PV strategy evaluation, first partial strategy verbalization, first branching adversarial explanation, or first use of Stockfish16 for strategy explanation.** Specific remaining methodological opening: finite sampled `k_eval` is not a universal legal-reply certificate; same-engine strategy construction/referee, no source-native NNUE evaluator→search/root mechanism, no proof a semantically SELECTIVE plan retains chess-value with independently verified reference and matched total costs. These are opportunities, not evidence of C3X superiority.

## Press & Golubev (2026), BinProp
Drive PDF `1gfmfug6814iYSEN3CkSF1xcsiG9uGTx6`, arXiv:2610.23166v1. Automatically propose Boolean Python chess motifs, evaluate 100,000 +125..250cp positions against 100,000 -250..-125cp positions, filter positive differential firing and overlap, then contrast candidate continuations and evaluate support, far negatives and nearby negatives. Table 1 contains eight-model results; best BinProp mean explainability 0.296 versus direct-prompt baselines at most 0.173. The metric is itself engine-grounded, and higher motif explainability correlates with Stockfish move ranking agreement (reported rho=0.66); does NOT establish true causal mediation of Stockfish internal search or independent chess positional truth. **C3X cannot claim novelty of executable Boolean motif discovery, foil comparisons, local-negative falsifiers, source-verifiable explanations, or engine-backed chess concepts alone.**

## Hebbar et al. (2026), ACT-Eval
Drive PDF `1BOHr8mZzY6vp14PBr2MrAiJH9vJDQStA`, arXiv:2608.04240v1. Atomic chess commentary claim decomposition → deterministic chess tools and chess/engine judgment, expert-annotated conceptual coverage, human calibration. **325 position–move benchmark** with **125 expert-gold subsets**, five error categories. GPT-5.4 without tools had 22.0% subclaim errors; with tools 9.2% in reported table. Calibration participants: **four chess players** with Lichess blitz 1800–2200, only two in coverage study; their tool still uses fallible judge judgments and completeness depends on expert gold. **C3X cannot claim pioneering atomic law/factual commentary audits, tool-assisted hallucination checking, or unique factual coverage metric.**

## Bhaskar, Cheng & Chen (2026), QUEEN
Drive PDF `1I1HikAacandg5b75R0zymJlgxAQ41gbG`, arXiv:2610.03695v1. 4B chess-language model integrating frozen Leela encoder and LM via learnable cross-attention; staged current/future legal and board query tasks, then iterative search-consolidated natural-language distillation across candidate branches. Reported seven updates, rating 1782→2697 based on **32 simulated games per model**, and NMR/FNMR on 1,000 tactical and 1,000 general positions; language coherence uses LM judging. **C3X cannot claim first language engine/LM bridge or first multi-candidate lookahead→natural-language distillation.** Rating and language-judge results do not independently prove faithful causal source explanations.

## Jenner et al. (2024), learned lookahead
Drive PDF `13QG6G_Ir67Q87Tkb1J9MOMn-TN4Zh63p`, final [NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/37d9f19150fce07bced2a81fc87d47a6-Abstract-Conference.html). Leela internal transformer attention and activation patching reveal future squares and causally consequential information, with reported probe predicting optimal move two turns ahead at 92% on a selected board subset. The paper's stated limitations: no precise implementation mechanism, focuses on ONE future line not multi-line search, and unusual puzzles. **C3X cannot merely claim discovering neural future-move representations or single-line internal causal effects**. Its distinct planned claim must be actual SF16 leaf/ancestor/root competitive search transmission and independent value prediction, not yet demonstrated.

## Schut et al. (2025), AlphaZero concept transfer
Drive PDF `1Mg4vfb2t-MIr6juCAzCC1qdv8sq1f8uj`, PNAS DOI:10.1073/pnas.2406675122. Identify AlphaZero latent concept vectors and filter by novelty/teachability, present puzzles to **four chess grandmasters** and observe improved concept-linked move choice in a preliminary study. **C3X cannot claim unique machine-concept novelty or first human chess teaching experiment.** A future C3X user study needs learner-skill conditioning and randomization, not an unsupported 'better human understanding' statement.

## Geiger et al. (2025), causal abstraction
Drive PDF `1dkkczKfLgmHL8YPwKWrKunEdCbAel6hK`, JMLR original. Defines causal abstraction, hard/soft and interchange interventions, path patching, mediation and approximate abstraction. **Observational branching logs and source-probe parity alone cannot show that neural features CAUSED a root move**; an intervention with correct mapping/interventional consistency is required.

## Parfenova (2026), explanation faithfulness
Drive PDF `1pNg13f4_OII1N1GUThSwB8I5ru_3Ns_R`, arXiv:2609.22245v1. 200 Lichess endgame puzzles, unmasked/masked move recoverability, decoder controls and token-level scoring. Explanations can leak the desired move and induce move preferences without improving correctness. **C3X Track B should mask literal move hints and evaluate actual quality separately from explanation recoverability.**

## Kim et al. (2024), CCC; Ruoss et al. (2024), ChessBench
Drive originals `1XpI5v0HJe_LNfa3HfKIlnEDVkJ-Fct74` and `1UBwx4nKx6i12tiHmqYZpAow644_Ts63x`. CCC already combines expert chess concept probes with language commentary and uses expert-informed commentary evaluation; explicitly notes insufficient novice-teaching validation. ChessBench has **10M games, ~15B annotated samples** from Stockfish 16; trained transformers up to 270M parameters to predict chess action-values without explicit search. Amortized chess-value prediction and high chess-playing strength are therefore established competitors, not a novelty of C3X.
