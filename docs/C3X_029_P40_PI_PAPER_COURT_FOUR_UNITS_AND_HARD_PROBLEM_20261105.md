## Paper candidate A — Source-Causal Chess Decision Explanations

Candidate thesis: exactly trace which Stockfish16 NNUE-derived internal value features were actually executed at a leaf, influenced alpha-beta cutoffs/TT and survived ancestors to change natural root candidate selection. P35 source-native instrument parity and 107129 actual Eval::evaluate calls across 8 contexts PASS, but NO feature-specific intervention/reliable ancestor-to-root mediation, only two exposed games. Original 2024 Leela learned-lookahead [Jenner et al NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/37d9f19150fce07bced2a81fc87d47a6-Abstract-Conference.html) already demonstrated causally active lookahead; 2026 [Sandmann et al](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21380) showed latent lookahead can be overridden by behavioral priors. Therefore representational discovery alone does not demonstrate the cause of final engine choice. **NOVELTY POSSIBLE, EMPIRICAL PROMOTION HOLD**.

## Paper candidate B — Counterfactual Plan Quality under Diverse Opponent Responses (FLAGSHIP)

Candidate thesis: a source-grounded value-bearing plan provides prospectively correct information beyond a strong Stockfish score+PV and equal-budget foil-aware explanations about what VALUE persists under different legal opponent replies. Must distinguish 'can execute plan' from 'plan preserves value'. Main unresolved hard challenge: how to define and independently adjudicate V_J(s,m,r,u) for multiple responses without using the predictor's own engine as ground truth or rewarding trivial plans. Prior [BinProp](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23166), [ACT-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04240), [ChessBench](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/78f0db30c39c850de728c769f42fc903-Abstract-Conference.html), and [Parfenova 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.blackboxnlp-1.21/) substantially constrain broad novelty claims. Historical P37 weak C3X-inspired 3-ply material predictor LOSES 4/15 vs PV 11/15 on SF16 96k reply oracle and 1/6 vs 4/6 on actual human reply. No separate full C3X predictor has achieved transfer. **HARD PROBLEM RETAINED, PAPER HOLD**.
