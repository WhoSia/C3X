## Central question
When competing legal first moves lead to different future game trees, which chess-relevant distinctions persist, extinguish, reunite, or reappear under declared opponent response assumptions; what is the smallest legally checkable witness establishing a specific distinction?

## Formal development contract
Let h be full game history (not merely FEN), s(h) its legal current board; a,b legal first actions. P declares opponent-response policy/reachability, H a finite continuation horizon, and V a preregistered chess predicate vocabulary. F(h,a;P,H,V) is a **proposal** for the set of legally replayable action/state histories projected through V under P to horizon H. Never equate a different set cardinality, Jaccard difference, FEN6 difference, engine score, or prose brevity to chess advantage or cause.

A witness certificate W=(h,a,b,P,H,p,t_a,t_b,s_a,s_b,claim,refuter) must include source chess history, legal contrasting continuations, chess-specific predicate p, clear evaluation directions, reachable state evidence, and a test that could falsify its claim. Invalid/missing witnesses => ABSTAIN.

Do not assume the survival of a distinction is monotone in horizon; one sample trajectory reunion does not imply game-tree equivalence; FEN6 omits repetition history. Same nominal UCI string on different states need not denote the same strategic consequence. For any mathematical 'geometry', define a distance/equivalence relation explicitly and check metric axioms or refrain from 'metric' claims.

## Rival accounts / scientific court
