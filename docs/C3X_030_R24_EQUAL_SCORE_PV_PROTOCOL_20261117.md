# R24 — A stable displayed preference can have different continuations

The public UCI score, best move, and principal variation are different observables. A PV is one reported legal line. Its moves give a witness to the search result and do not independently certify a plan or optimal play.

Use two fixed opening paths (the position after e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 and the Spanish position after Bb5 a6 Ba4 Nf6) and both previously exposed R23 game histories. Use the exact R23 upstream and instrumented Stockfish 16 builds, default NNUE, full legal roots, Threads 1, MultiPV 1 and fresh search process. Compare one continuous 300,000-node search with hash 16 MiB, and separate clean fixed-depth runs at depths 10, 12 and 14 with hash 16 and 64 MiB as the only changed setting within a depth pair. Check both binaries for normalized public output equality in every run. Pair only unbounded cp reports verified against an in-window native value from a completed root attempt.

For each trajectory, compare every pair of qualifying reports with identical first move and displayed cp. Mark identical PV, strict prefix length change, or conflicting moves at a shared ply. Record equality of the engine's raw value separately. Identify the mover and the first differing move. Report a same-ply equal full FEN if a divergence later rejoins within the recorded lines. A different line by itself warrants no claim about a changed strategy.

Use the first chronological pair in frozen case order whose raw values match and which differs within the first four moves after the recommendation. Freeze the pair before isolated reruns at its two nominal depths, and at each of its two reported node counts plus one. Compare final bestmove, last unbounded score and the early divergent move. In a separate check, judge the two divergent moves on an identical legal position with the pinned independent Berserk at depths 12 and 14, using a singleton legal root per run. Express values for the side that actually chooses that move.
