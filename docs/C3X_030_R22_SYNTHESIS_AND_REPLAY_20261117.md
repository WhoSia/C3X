# C3X 0.30 R22 — What should a move explanation predict?

The same legal choice can lead in a narrow search and leave an exposed worst reply. C3X's strategic target is to predict that divergence before the independent evaluation, identify the legal resource that actually changes the consequence, and show how the explanation reaches its user. The internal target is to determine which calculation changed the source estimate under a controlled contrast.

- [Board mechanism](C3X_030_R22_BB4_DEVELOPMENT_RESULTS_20261117.md). A legally replayable Bd2 line contributes after …Bb4. O-O is an alternative when Bd2 is removed and the majority of the conditioned gap survives. An explanation of only one attractive variation discards that evidence.
- [Reply policy and value](C3X_030_R22_REPLY_MENU_TRANSFER_RESULTS_20261117.md). The narrow source leader loses under the complete common-reply minimum in four of six depth-agreement games. Three reply-selection policies differ in coverage and reference ranking. Increasing source nodes on the same menus degrades rank forecasting in this regime. Selection and valuation require distinct measurements.
- [Native attempt bounds](C3X_030_R22_NATIVE_WINDOW_RESULTS_20261117.md). Certified thresholds describe an individual engine attempt. Twenty-five disagree with the eventual public endpoint at the same nominal depth. No additional ordering came from this reconstruction.
- [Study example](learner/C3X_R22_ROBUST_REPLY_EXAMPLE_KO.md). The renderer shows both candidates' hardest replies, relative direction and the measured error from limiting the reply list. The card can be regenerated with `product/c3x_reply_evidence.py SELECTION SCORE ROUND`. Human application still requires direct measurement.
