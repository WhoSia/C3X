## A. What the two positive cases actually establish

Historical Stage B native receipt and chess-forensics establish two distinct worlds with third-root outcomes:
- TWIC game4, world F, STRICT/BROAD: natural `c2c8` -> treated `c2d1`; O baseline `b5c3`. Divergent depth leaders at 9,10,12, not 11. The quiet queen move exposes 36 legal replies versus 5 after `c2c8` (queen capture/check), but reply count is neither evaluation nor engine motivation.
- TWIC game10, world O, STRICT/BROAD: natural `f8f7` -> treated `f4g3`; F baseline `a8e8`. Divergent depths 6,8,10,11,12, not 7 or 9. The third move is a pawn capture with legal recaptures `f2g3` and `h2g3`; descriptive relation is not source-level causal identification.

In opposite source-order worlds, game4 O takes the *other baseline* `c2c8`, and game10 F STRICT takes the *other baseline* `f8f7`. Thus third-candidate emergence is order-conditional in the measured developmental cases. The native receipt does not enumerate all raw actor paths; divergent depth leaders are not continuous mediator evidence.

**Sampling caveat:** 64 TWIC contacted role cells originate from 16 games. Four third-candidate role outcomes comprise two games × two source roles, not four independent chess positions. Historical cases were selected retrospectively. P9 does not estimate a general emergence rate from two games.

## B. Novel feedback: outcome equivalence is not mechanism equivalence

Outcome labels can coincide while the generating mechanism differs. Distinguish:
1. **First-contact mechanism:** which exact TT source reader/writer contact was blocked?
2. **Score/gate mechanism:** which bound/value, alpha/beta gate, or SEE pruning changed?
3. **Ancestor transmission:** was returned value actually consumed and promoted to root competition?
4. **Candidate fate:** was third move present but promoted, or previously pruned/absent and newly searched?
5. **Chess semantics:** are the chosen moves associated with demonstrably different legal responses or tactical properties?

The fifth cannot be inferred from the first four. Neither the outcome label nor earliest divergent depth identifies any of the first three.

## C. Competing source-level hypotheses with distinct falsifiers

| ID | Development hypothesis | Discriminating measurement | Falsification in specific case |
|---|---|---|---|
| H_TT | TT score/bound consumption changes a candidate ranking | exact writer/read, bound, score converted to node POV, parent consumption | no treated-versus-control differing consumed TT score on candidate-root ancestry |
| H_GATE | a search gate crossing changes candidate survival | alpha, beta, return score, bound type, cutoff at same source node | candidate switch with no differing gate/candidate-entry event in exhaustive traced scope |
| H_ORDER | move order or tie resolution dominates with no necessary score reversal | complete root move order, exact reported score intervals, tie resolution | strict contrary root margin retained with same order/tie information yet selected winner changes |
| H_ASP | aspiration retry or iteration carry-over transmits effect | retries, windows, RootMove initialization and update | same complete retry chronology and candidate states despite hypothesized mediation |
| H_SEE | SEE guard opens previously pruned continuation | source-exact SEE call, prune, child entry and parent update | no changed SEE event on trace-connected candidate path |

These are *testing hypotheses*, not claims that each mechanism has occurred in TWIC4/10. Absence is a falsifier only with a complete, source-aligned trace and verified noninterference; censorship/missing telemetry yields UNKNOWN.
