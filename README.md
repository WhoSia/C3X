## The scientific question

Can a chess concept be connected to an engine's **actual executable search mechanism** by legal chess certificates, precisely observed source events, matched micro-interventions and a disjoint held-out test — without equating a motif name with the engine's internal representation?

C3X studies **many-to-many concept–operator correspondences**:

- **Chess law and concrete tactics:** pin, skewer, discovered attack, interference, decoy, deflection, overloaded/removed defenders, clearance, fork, between-move tactics and opponent counterplay.
- **Position and strategy:** pawn structure, outposts, open lines, king shelter/activity, center control, initiative, simplification, long-horizon plan transitions and alternatives.
- **Engine software:** legal move generation, static exchange evaluation (SEE), capture/move ordering, quiescence search, stand-pat, physical transposition table (TT) writer/reader/bound/depth, alpha–beta/aspiration windows, root candidate competition and HCE/NNUE evaluation.

A geometric motif opportunity is **not** a certified tactic. A legal tactic is **not** proof that Stockfish used that human concept. A source event is **not** by itself a causal explanation. A model's final move is not a sufficient summary of its evaluation or computation.

## Why divide chess cases? Mechanism, not outcome convenience

The purpose of a case split is to **distinguish falsifiable source-level generating mechanisms**, not to manufacture small categories that fit already-seen root moves.

**Historical development example (not validation):** The two P6-disclosed TWIC worlds share a source-native SF16 TT-return treatment but **contact different qsearch paths**.

| Historical world | Distinguishing executable path | Why the contrast matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **#2 · CEFH** — Check-Evasion Fail-High Gate | TT early-return score 14; after its one-event bypass, legal check-evasion search yields child 173, β fail-high and target qsearch return 173 | The bypass exposes a new local value and a beta-break path; later P11 exact root relay clamp can restore the V-arm root outcome |
| **#29 · RISR** — Return-Invariant Search Reconfiguration | TT early-return 29; after bypass, qsearch computes stand-pat and legal continuations but also returns 29 | Same local scalar can conceal a different search history and changed root action; #29 is a **no-contact control for P11 relay**, not a scientifically empty case |

These are two **post-P6-outcome-selected development cases** — not two representative classes of chess concepts. Their difference is grounded in TT/qsearch **source execution**, not in a tactical label or a post-hoc root prediction.

To authorize a **new** case split: (1) freeze raw game/history/FEN groups and the proposed discriminator **before reading new outcomes**; (2) document chess-law/candidate move/counterdefense and the exact TT writer–reader/slot/key/bound/depth/α–β window or explicit UNKNOWN; (3) state a competing mechanism and a sham/no-contact falsifier; (4) measure root move, score, node count and root-candidate sequence **separately**; (5) demand source-/game-disjoint and ideally engine-disjoint repeat validation; (6) abstain if the split can only be justified by the response.
