C3X 0.12 -- Rival-First Explanation Precommit

Historical founding question: why one near-equal legal chess move is marginally preferred over another, including alternative legal responses, rather than merely narrating best moves.

Direct prior art: Cui et al. 2026 arxiv.org/abs/2607.11486; Kim et al. 2025 arxiv.org/abs/2410.20811, github.com/ml-postech/concept-guided-chess-commentary.

Precommit: freeze a legal move pair, engine regime, mover-perspective score and at least two rival strategic mechanisms before any explanatory outcomes. Check mate-score comparability; distinguish board-feature change from intervening on a putative cause. Select a non-mainline reply independently of observed outcome. Specify null/control and falsification condition. Explanatory text is downstream of typed evidence and may abstain. Concept-guided commentary, strategy-tree verbalization, human-move prediction and causal explanation are distinct comparators.

Conditional necessity and sufficiency are relative to legally admissible intervention families, not universal. Engine agreement is not a human-cognitive claim. Historic P23 FAIL, P24/P25 HOLD and Q0 DENIED remain unchanged. No 0.12 scientific result claimed.

Polyglot: native C++ UCI engines own search measurement; SQL for provenance joins where useful; Rust only if hard typed ledger adds real validity; Python limited to convenience preparation; GitHub Actions read-only.
