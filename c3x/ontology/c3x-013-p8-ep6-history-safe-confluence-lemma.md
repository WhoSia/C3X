## Scope problem: full FEN does NOT usually contain all chess history
Full FEN contains board occupation, side to move, castling rights, en-passant square, halfmove clock and fullmove number; it omits historical repetition counts. Thus in GENERAL, identical full FEN does not alone certify identical future FIDE threefold/fivefold repetition status. Do not silently equate generic full-FEN equality with complete history-bearing game-state identity.

## Special sufficient theorem for EP2/EP3 normal-vs-en-passant pawn confluences
Consider any STANDARD legal chess history ending in a root S with W pawns of the side P whose pawn will be captured. Two legal alternative moves a,b of that SAME pawn occur from S (for these audits, single push versus double push). In each branch the opponent immediately makes a legal capture of that JUST MOVED pawn (ordinary capture or en passant), and the two two-ply branches reach the EXACT same six-field FEN T.

**Lemma (pawn-count irreversibility and repetition-history separation).** In every legal earlier state of either branch up to but excluding the capture, side P had at least W pawns. In T, side P has W−1 pawns. In every legal future state reachable from T, side P has at most W−1 pawns. Consequently no legal future state after T can equal any earlier pre-capture state in either branch (pawn counts differ strictly). Both branches start from the same T and share the same FEN clock and move rights. For identical future moves, all future FIDE repetition occurrences arise solely in the common post-T suffix, and the initial prior histories introduce no discrepancy in repetition counts. Under ordinary chess game-state rules (ignoring external wall-clock, player psychology, adjudication, tablebase outside standard rules), these two conditional continuation game trees after that capture are identical for legal moves and board/draw conditions.

**Proof:** Pawn number of either color is nonincreasing under all STANDARD legal chess moves: pawn movement preserves it; captures reduce the victim's count if victim is a pawn; promotion reduces the promoting side's pawn count and never creates a pawn. At S the counted side has W. The single/double pawn push preserves W. The immediate opponent capture takes that same pawn, yielding W−1. Hence chronological earlier counts ≥W, and all subsequent counts ≤W−1. Equal chess positions must have equal piece occupation and pawn count, so earlier and later position repetition is impossible. Because the two endpoints T have exact equal full FEN, all castling, en-passant, side-to-move and 50/75 move clocks agree. Since no pre-T position can be repeated later, repetition-draw counting depends only on the common suffix and agrees. QED within listed assumptions.

**Limits:** This certifies history-SAFE **conditional** confluence for the 14 frozen EP3 witnesses only after checking the pawn capture victim and full-FEN identity; it does NOT assert source game move outcomes, opponent best replies, parent-root score equality, engine internal search state independence, human chess understanding, nor whether en passant is strategically relevant. A chess engine with different search budgets, history heuristics, TT, or move ordering can differ at PARENT roots even though the two conditional post-capture game states agree.
