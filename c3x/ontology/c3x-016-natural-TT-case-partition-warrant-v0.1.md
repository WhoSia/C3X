## Why a chess case is a scientific unit, not an after-the-fact tactic label

### 1. Primitive question

When should two chess position/search experiments receive different mechanism labels, and why may one contrast be more informative than an arbitrarily enlarged taxonomy?

A case is a **locked intervention opportunity plus its matchable source execution history**, not just a FEN or motif. Let
[
K=(P,H,	heta;G,O,W,R,Q,C,Y,V,N),
]
where (P,H) encode the board and game history; (	heta) includes source version, evaluator, depth and TT policy; (G) is chess-law opportunity; (O) is actual executing source operator; (W) is the TT physical writer and saved bound/depth/slot/key; (R) is the reader and consumer window; (Q) is a bounded continuation event trace; (C) is root candidate competition; (Y,V,N) are final move, score and node count. This is a **proposed evidence schema**, not a claim that all dimensions are identified.

**Admissible partition principle.** Distinguish cases only when the partition predicate
1. is defined from chess-law/source path *before new trial outcomes are inspected*;
2. selects a mechanically distinct branch/contact opportunity that could be independently challenged;
3. has explicit common-condition, sham, contact/noncontact, and competing mechanism controls;
4. groups same game/history/FEN and correlated source-events correctly rather than treating every node as an independent case;
5. reports the sample size, false-positive rate and abstention for each group; and
6. offers prospective power/transport justification, not merely increased training accuracy after slicing.

**A post-outcome counterexample split may aid mechanism discovery** but must remain labeled `RETROSPECTIVE_SOURCE_MECHANISM_EXPLANATION_ONLY` and cannot be promoted to new-cohort predictive support.

### 2. Physical natural TT graph versus downstream rescue

For a concrete source trace, represent actual computational descendants as a typed event DAG, not a line imposed by theory:
[
W_{mathrm{TT}}(k,slot,b,d,t)
	o R_{mathrm{TT}}(k,slot,alpha,eta,ply)
	o {V_{mathrm{return}}, Q_{mathrm{continue}}}
	o C_{mathrm{root}} 	o (Y,V,N).
]
Every drawn arrow is initially **SOURCE_ORDERED/CONTEXT_ONLY**, not automatically `MICROINTERVENTION_IDENTIFIED`. TT slot reuse and replacement, hash collisions, writer generations, transpositions, move-order updates and aspiration re-search create competing parents. A stored TT bound (EXACT/LOWER/UPPER) has search-dependent semantics: merely seeing a changed bound at a different key proves neither same-site mediation nor necessity.

To prove an **actual path effect**, preselect a real writer/reader/consumer event by full64 key, physical slot, writer tag/seq, bound/depth, consumer window/ply; preserve no-contact and sham runs; independently block/restore the exact event; and compare aligned downstream candidate changes. Need negative-control **same source key but no actual consumer read** and **consumer matched but return guard not taken** when feasible. A root value clamp proves an artificial relay may restore an outcome, not that the natural writer is a unique but-for cause.

- **#29 / RISR:** P8 bypass follows **not-in-check stand-pat29 → actual legal candidate continuations → qsearch returns29**, the SAME scalar as immediate TT value29; root behavior can still vary. These cases are separated by *measured source execution/continuation semantics*, not because one move looks like a pin/skewer or because the binary bestmove labels differ. They also refute a universal `TT-return value alone → root move` explanation under the fixed experiments. They DO NOT prove the same two strata exist as general natural populations of chess search mechanisms.

### 5. Case splitting for genuinely new 0.16 motifs

Skewer, interference, decoy, defender removal, pin and strategic plans may coexist in the SAME position and search. Do not make them exclusive classes by fiat. Use a **multi-label mechanism opportunity graph**. First declare source-only geometry/legality/actual opponent counters; secondly verify named source operator contact; thirdly intervene with negative controls; fourthly test external provider/depth/evaluator transport. When two concepts share the same causal source event and cannot be separated by any independent intervention, **do not split them into separate identified mechanisms**. When one named tactic triggers different executable paths under a predeclared discriminator, allow subdivision but require fresh disjoint examples before treating it as an established causal taxon.
