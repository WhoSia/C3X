## A. Distinguish three different contracts
1. **Lookup identity**: original `tt.cpp` stores the 16 low bits `key16` alongside the *cluster index* `mul_hi64(key, clusterCount)`. At 16 MiB, `ClusterSize=3`, `sizeof(Cluster)=32`, `clusterCount=524288=2^19`; so index depends on top 19 key bits, tag on bottom 16. Two distinct 64-bit keys can agree on BOTH while varying the 29 middle bits; the source does not check full key identity. Any fabricated keys are **abstract collision witnesses**, not certified realizable legal board Zobrist hashes or empirical rates.
2. **History/context**: original `Position::set_state()` hashes pieces, side, castling and en-passant, but NOT `rule50`/repetition counters. Original `Position::is_draw(ply)` checks `rule50 > 99` (subject to legal check/move condition) and repetition information; search/quiescence consults the draw predicate **before** TT probing. The same whole board `pos.key()` therefore cannot alone determine legal search behavior across different histories.
3. **Score meaning**: `BOUND_UPPER`, `BOUND_LOWER`, fail-soft search windows, qsearch heuristic pruning/stand pat, mate conversion and TT depth do not by themselves constitute a proof of exact minimax/finite-depth rival utility bounds. Under separately PROVEN score-interval premises, negamax transforms `[L,U]` to `[-U,-L]`, and root maximization of individually sound intervals is sound: `max_i[L_i,U_i]=[max_i L_i,max_i U_i]`.
