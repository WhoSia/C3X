### 1. Original TT eligibility and type barrier
Read actual SF16 (git `68e1e9b3811e16cad014b590d7443b9063b3eb52`) `src/search.cpp` and `src/tt.cpp`:
- `TTEntry::save` writes an internal search estimate with a BOUND_UPPER/LOWER/EXACT tag, plus a separate static evaluation, depending on the exact search source and overwrite rule. In qsearch, **stand pat** fail-high can save BOUND_LOWER without enumerating any legal move; and regular search includes null-move, LMR and futility pruning. Converting `BOUND_LOWER` in the table into *ground-truth chess-game utility ≥ beta* is not justified from tag/key/writer/read provenance alone. Even a FULL key match does not prove the writer's value, because of contextual draw/repetition differences.
- A source-local negative TT control and physical writer provenance is a `SourceTTRecord`, not `VerifiedGameOutcomeBound`; a separate semantics/legality proof must discharge this type conversion. Restrict any positive proof to a tiny explicit terminal-game witness first.
- Source provenance is a *justification of what happened*, not only the right to safely exclude candidate moves. Explainable Stockfish should give actual examples of possible/legal candidate continuations, demonstrations that certain outcomes cannot occur immediately, and (if proven) necessity of a property over all legal alternatives.
