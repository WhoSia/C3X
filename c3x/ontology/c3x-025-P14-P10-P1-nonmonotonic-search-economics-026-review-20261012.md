## 3. Mathematics: what the counterexample does, and does not, prove
Let `p` be a particular reachable chess search root, `b` a fixed nominal search depth budget, and `θ` a chosen original-source TT cutoff policy. Define *observed* deterministic tuples `F(p,θ,b)=(M(p,θ,b),N(p,θ,b),R(p,θ,b),C(p,θ,b))`, for native bestmove M, nodes N, aspiration retries R, relevant candidate competition observations C. The existing P14-P6 physical original source comparison refutes the **universal monotonic-work conjecture** `N(p,SkipTT,b)≥N(p,SHAM,b)` for its exactly defined single source intervention. It does *not* establish a generally smaller true engine cost (wall-clock CPU includes different calculation types), monotonic evaluation quality, causal identification of every intermediate search edge or correct chess policy selection. The four newer inputs show why a *contextual* interaction model is required.

Source-bound type and game-theoretic value should remain distinguished:
`ActualSourcePolicyContact` -> `ObservedSearchEffect` is a verified physical counterfactual relation for one exposure (with matching cold source controls).
`SourceTTRecord` -> `ExactChessUtilityBound` remains unproved. **The right goal is not universal cp proof but a sound, precise contract for whatever quantity a proposed policy actually uses**, possibly relative candidate survival, source-bound contact, effort, re-search scheduling or finite tactical response witnesses. Maintain factual, typed abstention when the named claim is unsupported.

## 4. Genuine explanation of outstanding, quiet or surprising chess moves
An elementary mate-in-one is not the target. A competitive future explanation requires a genuinely **nonterminal** decision where:
- an apparently plausible rival has a specific opponent counter-response, or a quiet move changes future obligations and source search priority;
- at least two real legal rivals are analyzed under controlled budgets and TT policies, including false leads and actual re-search;
- the claimed causal feature can be independently intervened on in original C++ and predicts *a specified observable* (not only a post hoc narrative);
- the explanation distinguishes **chess-semantic merit**, **search-process causality**, and **search-resource cost**. A large engine cp score or a chess site's aesthetic *brilliant move* label by itself proves neither explanation nor novelty.

A pragmatic research strategy is *rival-directed search allocation*: reserve a fixed node/time comparison budget; use originally observed uncertainty, failed aspiration, candidate survival or independent source-verified tactical reply to allocate the next original search to the genuinely threatening candidate. Preserve a **competitive matched-budget baseline**, calibration of abstention, legal-move and repetition tests, and falsifying cases where the adviser makes decisions worse. It is a **proposed research controller, not an implemented result**.
