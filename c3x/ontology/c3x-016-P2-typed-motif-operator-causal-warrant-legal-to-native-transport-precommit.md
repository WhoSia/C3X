## Primitive question P2
For a concrete motif m (e.g. skewer, interference, defender removal, decoy, strategic outpost plan) and an actual Stockfish operator o (legality, SEE, qsearch, capture ordering, root aspiration, TT writer/reader, PSQ/NNUE), which **independent pieces of evidence** justify asserting m was causally relevant to a root choice, rather than merely that m's board geometry co-occurred with o's execution?

Define the **typed motif-operator edge** (proposal, not universal law):
\[
E_{m,o}(P,H,\theta, a)
 = \big[L_{m},\,R_{m},\,O_{o},\,I_{m,o},\,Y,\,V,\,N,\,D,\,T\big].
\]
L=chess-law certificate, R=concrete opponent defense / alternative continuation, O=source event/physical TT provenance, I=source preselected intervention contact and sham, Y=final categorical move, V=reported score, N=nodes, D=earliest safely aligned root event, T=transport source/engine status.

These are typed dimensions, NOT one scalar progress score. No motif→operator one-to-one identity or assumption that an NNUE learned unit encodes the human name.
