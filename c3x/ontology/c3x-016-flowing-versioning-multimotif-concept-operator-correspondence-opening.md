## 1. Primitive question and genuinely new object

0.15 investigated **how a preselected pinned source object and one native SEE/TT/qsearch event can change Stockfish16's search/choice**. C3X 0.16 asks instead:

> Can we give separately checked, discriminative and transportable causal certificates linking multiple lawful chess motifs and strategic plans to exact executable search operators, WITHOUT mistaking motif names, root choice, engine intentions, or artificial evaluation interventions for the same kind of evidence?

C3X 0.16 focuses on a correspondence **relation**, not a PIN-centric equivalence. Skewer, interference, clearance, deflection, defender removal, discovered attack, fork, decoy, overloaded defenders and zwischenzug are **candidate tactical sequences**, requiring real legal move continuations and counterdefenses. Pawn structure, passed pawn, outposts, open files, central control, king safety/activity, simplification and prophylaxis are **strategy hypotheses**, requiring multiple positions/horizon and alternative plans. A one-position detector is an opportunity feature, NOT a certified tactic or validated plan.

A motif can span several source operators, and one source operator (e.g. SEE or TT) can serve many motifs. Model as typed many-to-many relation rather than a forced class label.
