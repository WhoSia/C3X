## 1. Mathematical theorem and countermodel
Let machine states be S, observed chess-state projection be π:S→B, a fixed-policy deterministic terminal action be f:S→M, and an original/state-intervention transition T:S→S. A function g:B→M explaining the present action exists iff f is constant on π-fibers. A deterministic projected transition T̄ satisfying π∘T=T̄∘π exists iff π(s)=π(t) implies π(T(s))=π(T(t)). The second condition does not follow from the first. For a family U of interventions, demand this implication for every admissible T_u, not merely one observed final UCI.

Executable C++17 finite countermodel in tools/c3x_025_P13_R4_dynamic_transition_quotient_court.cpp enumerates 16 toy states with visible board/history and hidden writer/generation bits. The present action always factors through visible board/history (zero present-action conflicting fibers), yet four public fibers have two possible visible successor states because T reads hidden writer. A sham ignores writer and preserves the public quotient. Enriching observation by the one writer bit restores congruence for the modeled transition, while hidden generation remains irrelevant in this toy. Locally compiled GNU C++17 with -Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic -O2; 16-state full enumeration, four next-transition fiber conflicts, zero sham conflicts, zero enriched conflicts. DO NOT claim that the real Stockfish machine needs or is sufficed by a single bit.

Prior-art rivals already available in shared Research OS Commons: Cousot & Cousot (1977) abstract interpretation; Sangiorgi (2008) bisimulation and coinduction; Geiger et al. (2025) Causal Abstraction. C3X is not claiming novelty for elementary fiber-factorization or transition congruence. Its falsifiable ambition is source-aligned dynamic intervention classes and true behavioral changes in real search.
