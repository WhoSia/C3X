## Pure mathematics and TCS
For a **nonempty sound feasible family** K of jointly comparable root candidate value vectors and fixed candidate set A, strict winner w is certified exactly when every feasible vector satisfies `v(w)>v(a)` for every rival a. If K is compact, equivalently each pairwise minimal difference is strictly positive. Under nonempty information refinement K'⊆K a sound certificate remains true. Coordinate projection to per-move intervals loses score correlations: `K={(1,0),(2,1)}` certifies first move's margin 1, yet marginal intervals [1,2] and [0,1] intersect and the rectangular strict-gap method abstains. The relationship is the object of study, not a single confidence scalar.
