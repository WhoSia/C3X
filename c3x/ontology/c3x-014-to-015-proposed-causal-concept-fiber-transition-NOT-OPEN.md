## Why this is a substantively new phase

C3X 0.14 established operational many-to-many correspondence rather than one-to-one human concepts → engine internals:
- The human name "pin" is associated with at least four distinct real original Stockfish16 source operations: king-ray legal move constraints, SEE pinned recapture eligibility, classical king-blocker mobility, classical WeakQueen own-/enemy-ray vulnerabilities.
- Official TCEC S29 source-only chess law corpus includes 702 king-absolute pin witnesses and **31** observed composite law subtype keys (king/pinned/pinner ray, piece types, actual pinned legal mobility, pinner capture).
- Source-frozen 40 selected worlds contain **30 law subtype keys**; only **four repeated-law groups / 14 distinct source game worlds** support same-law comparisons, a strong limit on statistical transport claims.
- Actual SF16 native executed whole-tree operation signatures differ in 3/4 NNUE-setting and 1/4 CLASSICAL-setting repeated law groups, but exact-root naturally observed operator calls are often censored and give 0/4 repeated-law discrimination.
- Explicit root diagnostic (run #37890914822) performed **80 SOURCE-NATIVE NO-SEARCH engine processes** and 40/40 chess legality validation. Legal pinned move tested 31/40; actually rejected 24/40; root SEE masked 1/40; classic mobility king-blocker 38/40; root WeakQueen 4/40. Repeated law group with explicit root binary signature divergence: 1/4. These are scheduled *opportunity measurements*, not naturally selected search events.
- **Actual direct five-arm source intervention** (run #37891155659): 40 original legal root worlds, CLASSICAL evaluator, depth12, two cold starts, **400 real SF16 native processes**, OFF exactly reproduces previously verified original native output and counters **40/40**; cold 200/200; suppressing a code branch that did not fire never changes the UCI output.
- SEE pinned recapture filter knockout changes root bestmove in 11/40 source worlds; WeakQueen own blocker penalty knockout 8/40, enemy blocker 9/40, combined 13/40.
- The source worlds exhibit **11 distinct empirical four-bit root bestmove response signatures** out of 16 possible. Distribution (bits ordered SEE_UNMASK, WQ_OWN, WQ_ENEMY, WQ_BOTH):
  `0000:22, 0001:2, 0010:2, 0011:1, 0100:1, 0101:1, 1000:2, 1001:2, 1011:1, 1101:1, 1111:5`.
  **18/40** respond to at least one direct code intervention; 4 source roots respond to BOTH WeakQueen components together but neither separately; nonzero four-arm classical cp finite-difference interaction in **28/40** (range −36..+54cp, can be caused by changing search topology rather than direct evaluation term interaction).
- Among four *same chess-law* root subtype groups, THREE show distinct four-bit causal response signatures. This is genuine whole-search software operator **causal sensitivity heterogeneity**, *not evidence of 11 stored Stockfish NNUE tactical neurons, pin-specific root mediation, or cross-source concepts*.

## Proposed future question and explicit admission requirements for C3X 0.15

**Research question:** Can chess tactical/strategic concepts be organized as a stable, refineable partial-order of causal **engine-operator fibers** that explains why geometrically similar positions invoke different methods and alter move preference, and does that taxonomy transport beyond source games and engines?

**To OPEN 0.15**, require, before reading new outputs:
- Frozen new independent source game cohort not overlapping existing 100 TCEC S29 official games or the existing source 14 games/28 worlds. Preserve game-source IDs, full legal histories, raw PGNs/byte hashes, and honest selection denominators.
- Source-grounded operator interventions that *target the root tactical object or chess feature exposure*, not just globally alter all search nodes. Preserve legal move generation. Separate diagnostic root opportunity calls, organic root/site trace, and actual search-wide causal treatment.
- Repeated same-geometry and same-law subtype groups across **different games and provider/engine** to challenge whether the original 11 sensitive 4-bit patterns transport rather than just overfit source. No independent source support → leave 0.14 HOLD and do not announce stable latent classes.
- Alternative/nonpin controls, matched search depth vs completedDepth and actual nodes, multiple fixed budgets and root candidate comparisons. Rule out simple "more computation" confounding and NNUE/classic evaluator mixing via proven PSQ fallback.
- At least one prospective *positive* and one *negative* mechanism subtype criterion verified in source-disjoint worlds and, ideally, a separate engine family; separate each level of claims `source code branch`, `root causal mechanism`, `cross-source invariant`, `NNUE latent representation`, `human interpretability`.
- For NNUE learned latent categories specifically, inspect controlled NNUE feature changes/activations at matched positions and rule out mere static material/board context signal. A set of 11 engine *output* signatures does not suffice.

**Possible expansion beyond pins**, as untested source-grounded targets requiring their own source law/features and interventions: legal vs relative pin, discovered attack, discovered check, double attack, overloading, deflection, interference, X-ray pin/skewer, exchange sacrifice, zugzwang, fortress and perpetual-check pressure. These are *prospective human term families*, not claimed discovered engine categories.

**0.16 is not proposed as an active stage**. Only later, after substantive cross-source/engine and operator-faithfulness success, consider a truly distinct human-facing explanation-validation research contract.
