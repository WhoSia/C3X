## Proposed future question and explicit admission requirements for C3X 0.15

**Research question:** Can chess tactical/strategic concepts be organized as a stable, refineable partial-order of causal **engine-operator fibers** that explains why geometrically similar positions invoke different methods and alter move preference, and does that taxonomy transport beyond source games and engines?

**To OPEN 0.15**, require, before reading new outputs:
- Frozen new independent source game cohort not overlapping existing 100 TCEC S29 official games or the existing source 14 games/28 worlds. Preserve game-source IDs, full legal histories, raw PGNs/byte hashes, and honest selection denominators.
- Source-grounded operator interventions that *target the root tactical object or chess feature exposure*, not just globally alter all search nodes. Preserve legal move generation. Separate diagnostic root opportunity calls, organic root/site trace, and actual search-wide causal treatment.
- Repeated same-geometry and same-law subtype groups across **different games and provider/engine** to challenge whether the original 11 sensitive 4-bit patterns transport rather than just overfit source. No independent source support → leave 0.14 HOLD and do not announce stable latent classes.
- Alternative/nonpin controls, matched search depth vs completedDepth and actual nodes, multiple fixed budgets and root candidate comparisons. Rule out simple "more computation" confounding and NNUE/classic evaluator mixing via proven PSQ fallback.
- At least one prospective *positive* and one *negative* mechanism subtype criterion verified in source-disjoint worlds and, ideally, a separate engine family; separate each level of claims `source code branch`, `root causal mechanism`, `cross-source invariant`, `NNUE latent representation`, `human interpretability`.
- For NNUE learned latent categories specifically, inspect controlled NNUE feature changes/activations at matched positions and rule out mere static material/board context signal. A set of 11 engine *output* signatures does not suffice.

**Possible expansion beyond pins**, as untested source-grounded targets requiring their own source law/features and interventions: legal vs relative pin, discovered attack, discovered check, double attack, overloading, deflection, interference, X-ray pin/skewer, exchange sacrifice, zugzwang, fortress and perpetual-check pressure. These are *prospective human term families*, not claimed discovered engine categories.

**0.16 is not proposed as an active stage**. Only later, after substantive cross-source/engine and operator-faithfulness success, consider a truly distinct human-facing explanation-validation research contract.
