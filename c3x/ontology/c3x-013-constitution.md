## Distinct evidentiary levels
- Level F: observable board fact, such as a piece's attack on one of four named candidate squares; legal FEN and source checks required.
- Level P: descriptive chess-concept *partition* defined by a stable rule and semantic falsifiers across disjoint board worlds.
- Level M: intervention-sufficient mechanism proven prospectively with legal, matched controls, exact engine/budget and independent sources. **Initially unavailable**.
- Level T: cross-engine and continuation transport; not inferred from one Stockfish lineage.
- Level H: human understanding benefit; only genuine randomized participant rows, never simulated survey responses.

Never promote F to P, P to M, M to T or T to H by labels or a JSON schema alone.
