**Suggested subject:** Research reuse permission for limited TWIC chess-position evidence

Dear Mr Crowther,

I am conducting an independent research project about causal interventions in the Stockfish chess search engine. I have used The Week in Chess tournament PGN archives (including issues 1656 and 1664) to select a small number of legal chess positions for controlled engine experiments. I appreciate the work involved in collecting these tournament records.

I noted that TWIC's archive is provided for personal use and all rights are reserved. I am therefore not distributing the original PGN files or ZIP archives. Before publicly releasing any TWIC-derived experiment data, I would like to ask whether you would permit the following uses, and on what attribution or licence terms:

1. Publishing a small selection of individually identified chess positions in FEN notation and one associated played-move UCI label per position (about 16 positions per issue).
2. Publishing engine-generated evaluations and interventions linked to those positions, excluding player names and full source-game move lists.
3. Sharing only aggregated counts and SHA-256 checksums, together with research scripts that require an investigator to obtain TWIC independently.
4. If permissible, distributing a small reproducibility supplement containing the selected FEN/played-move records, but **not** complete PGNs or the complete TWIC database.

Would you be comfortable with any of these release levels, and what precise acknowledgement, redistribution conditions, or scope limits would you prefer? I can provide a sample supplement and repository link for review before publication.

Thank you for considering this request.

Best regards,
C3X independent research project
